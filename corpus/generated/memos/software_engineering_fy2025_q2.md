## **Executive Summary**  
The Software Engineering department reported total expenses of $11.54M in FY2025 Q2, exceeding the budget of $7.96M by $3.58M (+45.1%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$2.34M, +45.2%), Technology investments (+$657K, +41.3%), and Professional Services (+$317K, +49.8%). The variance reflects strategic hiring initiatives, expanded project demands, and increased vendor reliance to support key deliverables. While these investments align with our growth strategy, cost overruns necessitate closer monitoring in future quarters.  

---

## **Expense Performance by Category**  

1. **People Costs**: Actuals reached $7.52M, exceeding the $5.18M budget by $2.34M (+45.2%).  
2. **Technology**: Expenses totaled $2.25M, surpassing the $1.59M budget by $657K (+41.3%).  
3. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $954K were $317K (+49.8%) above budget.  
4. **Travel & Expense**: Spending of $591K exceeded the $393K budget by $198K (+50.3%).  
5. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Costs of $227K were $71K (+45.8%) over budget.  

Every category reported unfavorable variances, with People Costs contributing 65% of the total overage.  

---

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  

1. **People Costs (+$2.34M, +45.2%)**:  
   The variance is attributed to an aggressive hiring push to support the launch of the new AI-driven portfolio management platform. Over 40 new roles were filled, including high-cost senior engineering positions, driving increased reliance on LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($2.99M) and Indeed.com ($2.92M). Additionally, onboarding costs and expanded benefits through Principal Financial Group ($2.27M) and Aetna Health Insurance ($2.09M) contributed to the overage.  

2. **Technology (+$657K, +41.3%)**:  
   Higher-than-expected investments in cloud infrastructure and software tools were necessary to scale development environments for the AI platform. These included unplanned upgrades to Workday systems ($2.33M) and additional licenses for collaboration tools.  

3. **Professional Services (+$317K, +49.8%)**:  
   The department engaged external consultants to accelerate development timelines and address skill gaps in machine learning and cybersecurity. These services were critical to meeting key project milestones but exceeded initial projections.  

4. **Travel & Expense (+$198K, +50.3%)**:  
   Increased travel for cross-functional collaboration, particularly between engineering hubs in New York and San Francisco, drove this overage. Travel also supported recruiting efforts and vendor negotiations.  

5. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$71K, +45.8%)**:  
   Additional office space was leased to accommodate new hires, driving higher facilities costs.
