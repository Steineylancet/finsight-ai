### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q4, the Sales - Americas team is forecasted to exceed its budget by $5.53M (+8.3%), with total forecasted spend reaching $71.96M. This variance is primarily driven by anticipated overperformance in Renewals (+11.9%) and New Business (+7.0%), both of which reflect strong market demand and effective execution of sales strategies. While most expense categories are forecasted to remain within a narrow range of budget, incremental investments in Consulting (+4.7%) and Professional Services (+2.7%) are expected to support critical client engagements and strategic initiatives.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **Renewals**: Forecasted at $37.00M, exceeding budget by $3.94M (+11.9%). This reflects a robust renewal pipeline, with several high-value contracts expected to close in Q4.  
- **New Business**: Projected at $16.77M, surpassing budget by $1.09M (+7.0%). Continued momentum in new client acquisition is driving this growth, supported by targeted marketing campaigns and expanded sales capacity.  
- **Professional Services & Consulting**: Combined, these categories are forecasted to exceed budget by $429K (+3.3%), reflecting increased demand for client implementation and advisory services.  
- **People Costs**: Expected to reach $2.76M, a modest increase of $66K (+2.4%), driven by planned hires to support Q4 growth initiatives.  
- **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted to remain flat at $1.11M (-0.2%), reflecting disciplined cost management despite increased client-facing activity.  
- **Technology & Facilities**: Both categories are forecasted slightly below budget (-1.6% and -0.4%, respectively), reflecting cost optimization efforts.  
- **Marketing**: Projected at $563K, a slight increase of $11K (+1.9%), tied to end-of-year campaigns aimed at driving both renewals and new business.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
1. **Renewals Momentum**: A strong Q4 renewal pipeline includes several large contracts with favorable terms, supported by proactive client engagement strategies.  
2. **New Business Growth**: Expanded sales capacity and targeted marketing are expected to drive higher conversion rates, particularly in key verticals.  
3. **Strategic Investments**: Incremental spend in Consulting and Professional Services reflects the need to deliver on complex client engagements and maintain service quality.  
4. **Seasonality**: Q4 historically experiences elevated activity levels, particularly in Renewals and New Business, as clients finalize year-end budgets.  

---
