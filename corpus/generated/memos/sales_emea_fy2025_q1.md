### **Executive Summary**  
The EMEA Sales department closed FY2025 Q1 with total expenses of $26.29M, exceeding the budget of $24.13M by $2.16M (+9.0%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs, Renewals, and Travel & Expense categories. While some of the overspend reflects strategic investments in talent acquisition and client engagement, tighter cost controls will be necessary in subsequent quarters to align with annual targets.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
- **Renewals:** Actuals of $12.87M exceeded the budget of $11.87M by $994.6K (+8.4%).  
- **People Costs:** Actuals of $1.80M exceeded the budget of $1.27M by $533.3K (+42.0%).  
- **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $695.1K exceeded the budget of $497.7K by $197.4K (+39.7%).  
- **Marketing:** Actuals of $360.0K exceeded the budget of $251.0K by $109.0K (+43.4%).  
- **Facilities & Occupancy:** Actuals of $359.8K exceeded the budget of $251.7K by $108.1K (+42.9%).  
- **Technology:** Actuals of $356.4K exceeded the budget of $256.6K by $99.8K (+38.9%).  
- **Consulting & Professional Services:** Both categories remained close to budget with variances of +4.2% and +2.4%, respectively.  
- **New Business:** Actuals of $5.09M were slightly under budget by $22.3K (-0.4%).  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+42.0%, +$533.3K):**  
   The variance reflects a strategic hiring push to support regional growth initiatives, particularly in high-demand markets like Germany and the UAE. Top vendors, including Udemy Business ($704.3K) and LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($509.2K), contributed to upskilling and recruitment efforts. Additionally, onboarding costs for new hires were higher than anticipated.  

2. **Renewals (+8.4%, +$994.6K):**  
   Renewals exceeded budget due to higher-than-expected contract renegotiations with key clients. While this reflects a positive trend in client retention, the associated increase in commission payouts drove costs above plan.  

3. **Travel & Expense (+39.7%, +$197.4K):**  
   Increased travel activity, particularly for client meetings and regional sales conferences, contributed to the overspend. This aligns with a post-pandemic return to in-person engagements, which were critical for securing renewals and new business.
