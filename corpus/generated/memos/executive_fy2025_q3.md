### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2025 Q3, the Executive Department’s total expenses reached $2,759,565, exceeding the budget of $1,975,045 by $784,519 (+39.7%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs (+$458,628, +38.4%), Travel & Expense (+$153,022, +43.9%), and Professional Services (+$72,511, +37.0%). Key drivers include strategic investments in talent acquisition, leadership development initiatives, and increased executive travel related to business expansion efforts. While these expenditures reflect alignment with our long-term growth priorities, they underscore the need for tighter cost controls in future quarters.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  

1. **People Costs**: Actuals of $1,652,893 exceeded the budget of $1,194,265 by $458,628 (+38.4%). This category accounted for 60% of total expenses, driven by increased spending on recruitment, healthcare benefits, and leadership training programs.  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $501,676 were $153,022 (+43.9%) over budget. The variance reflects a rise in executive travel to support global expansion initiatives and client engagement efforts.  
3. **Professional Services**: Spending reached $268,515, exceeding the budget by $72,511 (+37.0%). This overage stems from consulting fees related to strategic planning and organizational restructuring.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Costs of $203,115 were $64,442 (+46.5%) above budget, driven by temporary office expansions to accommodate new leadership hires and offsite strategy sessions.  
5. **Insurance & Risk**: Actuals of $133,366 exceeded the budget by $35,917 (+36.9%), reflecting higher premiums associated with expanded executive coverage and risk mitigation measures.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  

- **People Costs**: The largest variance (+$458,628) was driven by two key factors: (1) a strategic push to onboard senior-level talent, reflected in $402,850 spent with LinkedIn Talent Solutions, and (2) increased healthcare costs, with $392,655 paid to Cigna Healthcare and $387,963 to Aetna Health Insurance. Additionally, $363,791 was allocated to Udemy Business for leadership development programs, underscoring our commitment to upskilling the executive team.  
- **Travel & Expense**: The $153,022 overage reflects a surge in international travel by senior leaders to support global expansion efforts in APAC and EMEA markets. These trips were critical for client relationship management and market-entry strategy development.  
- **Professional Services**: The $72,511 variance was tied to consulting fees for external advisors engaged in the ongoing organizational restructuring initiative. While this is a one-time cost, it is necessary to position the company for future growth.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy**: Temporary office expansions and offsite strategy meetings contributed to the $64,442 overage. These investments were necessary to accommodate leadership team growth and facilitate strategic planning.
