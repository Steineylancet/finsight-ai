### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
The 100% favorable variance across all categories is directly tied to the company’s strategic decision to implement a temporary operational pause in Finance Operations during Q4. This pause was part of a broader organizational restructuring to streamline processes, optimize resource allocation, and align departmental objectives with long-term corporate goals. Key drivers include:  

1. **People Costs (-$1,106,213):** The largest variance reflects the temporary redeployment of Finance Operations staff to other departments or projects during the restructuring. This measure allowed the company to avoid incurring payroll and benefits expenses within this cost center for the quarter.  

2. **Professional Services (-$387,005):** Vendor engagements for consulting, auditing, and other professional services were deferred as part of the operational pause. Contracts are expected to resume in FY2026 Q1, with renegotiated terms to align with updated departmental priorities.  

3. **Technology (-$238,470):** Planned software upgrades and system enhancements were postponed, pending the outcome of the IT infrastructure review currently underway.  

4. **Travel & Expense (-$99,522):** The suspension of non-essential travel and in-person meetings during the quarter contributed to this variance.  

5. **Facilities & Occupancy (-$158,211):** With the Finance Operations team operating in a remote or redeployed capacity, no costs were incurred for office space or related overhead.  

---

### **Key Vendors**  
No vendor transactions occurred during FY2025 Q4, as all vendor engagements were temporarily suspended. The department is in the process of reviewing vendor contracts to identify opportunities for cost savings and efficiency improvements in FY2026.
