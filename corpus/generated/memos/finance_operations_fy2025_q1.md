## Executive Summary  
The Finance Operations department closed FY2025 Q1 with total expenses of $2,120,483, exceeding the budget of $1,492,655 by $627,828 (+42.1%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs, Professional Services, and Technology expenses. These increases reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition, system upgrades, and consulting support for ongoing transformation initiatives. While these costs exceeded projections, they align with the department's broader objectives to support Crestwood Capital Group’s growth trajectory.

## Key Variances & Commentary  
1. **People Costs (+$328,898 / +40.0%):**  
   The largest variance stemmed from increased investments in talent acquisition to support the company’s growth. Notably, $402,987 was spent with Indeed.com, $384,376 with Cornerstone OnDemand, and $357,558 with LinkedIn Talent Solutions. These platforms were leveraged to fill critical roles in data analytics, compliance, and technology functions. Additionally, higher-than-expected health insurance costs with Aetna ($350,380) and Cigna ($346,990) reflect both increased headcount and a mid-quarter adjustment to premium rates.  

2. **Professional Services (+$137,672 / +45.8%):**  
   This variance was driven by consulting engagements tied to the implementation of a new financial reporting framework. External advisors were engaged to ensure compliance with evolving regulatory requirements and to streamline reporting processes ahead of the FY2025 audit cycle.  

3. **Technology (+$77,388 / +43.5%):**  
   The department invested in system upgrades, including enhanced cybersecurity measures and cloud-based financial tools. These initiatives were necessary to address vulnerabilities identified in Q4 FY2024 and to improve operational efficiency.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$52,209 / +44.3%):**  
   The variance reflects higher leasing costs for additional office space to accommodate new hires. This was compounded by inflationary pressures on utility and maintenance expenses.  

5. **Travel & Expense (+$31,661 / +43.0%):**  
   Increased travel was driven by leadership participation in industry conferences and client-facing engagements. The department also incurred costs for team-building events aimed at onboarding new employees.
