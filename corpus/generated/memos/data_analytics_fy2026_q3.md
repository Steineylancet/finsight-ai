## Executive Summary  
The Data & Analytics department is projected to close FY2026 Q3 with total expenses of $544,821, exceeding the budget of $533,620 by $11,200 (+2.1%). The variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs and Technology expenses, partially offset by modest savings in Professional Services and Travel & Expense. This forecast reflects the department’s strategic focus on scaling core capabilities to support enterprise-wide data initiatives while managing discretionary spending.  

---

## Planned Spend by Category  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $329,726, exceeding the budget by $9,130 (+2.8%). This increase reflects planned headcount growth, including two mid-quarter hires for data engineering roles to support the ongoing migration to a cloud-based data warehouse.  
2. **Technology**: Projected at $135,690, $3,665 (+2.8%) above budget. The variance is driven by a mid-year renewal of advanced analytics software licenses, which includes incremental costs for expanded user access and additional features.  
3. **Professional Services**: Expected to come in at $53,910, under budget by $913 (-1.7%). Lower-than-expected reliance on external consultants for the machine learning pilot contributed to this reduction.  
4. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $25,495, $682 (-2.6%) below budget. This reflects reduced in-person training sessions, with a shift to virtual formats for key workshops.  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  
- **Headcount Expansion**: The forecast assumes the addition of two full-time employees in Q3 to meet project milestones for the cloud migration initiative.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals for key software platforms, including analytics tools and data visualization licenses, are factored into the forecast. These tools are essential for supporting enterprise reporting needs.  
- **Professional Services Optimization**: Internal resources are being leveraged to manage certain project deliverables, reducing reliance on external consultants.  
- **Seasonal Adjustments**: Travel expenses are expected to remain subdued due to a continued emphasis on virtual collaboration.  

---

## Risks & Watch Items  
1. **Hiring Delays**: Any delays in onboarding the planned hires could impact project timelines and reduce People Costs relative to the forecast.  
2. **Technology Cost Escalation**: Unanticipated price adjustments during software renewals could further increase Technology expenses.  
3. **Project Dependencies**: The machine learning pilot relies on timely deliverables from cross-functional teams. Delays could necessitate additional external support, impacting Professional Services costs.  

We will continue to monitor these factors closely and provide updates as new information becomes available. The department remains committed to balancing cost discipline with the investments needed to support Crestwood Capital Group’s strategic objectives.
