## **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q3, the Executive Department’s total forecasted spend is $2,153,585, representing a 1.4% ($30,521) increase over the approved budget of $2,123,064. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs and Travel & Expense, partially offset by savings in Professional Services and Facilities & Occupancy. The forecast reflects planned strategic initiatives, including leadership team expansion, increased travel for stakeholder engagement, and cost optimization efforts in non-core areas.

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $1,295,275, exceeding the budget by $27,797 (+2.2%). This reflects planned hiring of two senior executives to support growth initiatives and anticipated merit adjustments.  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Projected at $400,672, surpassing the budget by $12,060 (+3.1%). The increase accounts for additional travel related to investor roadshows and leadership participation in industry conferences.  
3. **Professional Services**: Expected to come in at $206,176, under budget by $6,438 (-3.0%). Savings are driven by reduced reliance on external consultants as internal resources take on more strategic projects.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $144,361, below budget by $5,861 (-3.9%). This reflects ongoing efforts to optimize office space utilization and renegotiate vendor contracts.  
5. **Insurance & Risk**: Projected at $107,100, exceeding the budget by $2,962 (+2.8%). The increase is tied to higher premiums on D&O insurance, reflecting market trends and expanded coverage needs.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Strategic Hiring**: The forecast incorporates the onboarding of two senior executives in Q3 to support leadership bandwidth and strategic growth initiatives.  
- **Increased Travel**: Leadership’s expanded focus on external stakeholder engagement, including investor relations and partnership development, is expected to drive higher travel expenses.  
- **Cost Optimization**: Savings in Professional Services and Facilities & Occupancy are attributed to ongoing efforts to streamline operations and reduce discretionary spend.  
- **Insurance Premium Adjustments**: The forecast reflects updated market conditions for insurance coverage, particularly in risk management policies critical for executive functions.  

---

## **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Hiring Timelines**: Delays in onboarding planned hires could impact People Costs and potentially shift related expenses to future quarters.  
2. **Travel Volatility**: Unanticipated changes in travel needs or cost fluctuations (e.g., airfare, lodging) could further impact Travel & Expense forecasts.  
3. **Insurance Market Trends**: Continued upward pressure on premiums may necessitate additional adjustments in future quarters.  
4. **Vendor Negotiations**: Assumed savings in Facilities & Occupancy are contingent
