### **Executive Summary**  
The Marketing - Americas department closed FY2026 Q1 with total expenses of $3,952,248, exceeding the budget of $2,726,914 by $1,225,334 (+44.9%). This variance was driven by strategic investments in marketing initiatives, headcount expansion, and increased reliance on professional services and technology to support growth objectives. While the overspend is unfavorable, it reflects our proactive approach to capitalize on market opportunities and enhance brand visibility in a competitive landscape. Key vendor partnerships, including Cvent Inc ($507,869) and Marketo ($498,801), contributed significantly to these efforts.

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **Marketing (+$475,931 / +43.1%)**  
   The variance reflects increased spending on large-scale campaigns, including a new product launch targeting the Americas market. Investments in digital advertising through LinkedIn Marketing ($420,501) and enhanced event management services with Cvent Inc ($507,869) were key drivers. These initiatives aim to solidify Crestwood’s market presence and generate long-term revenue growth.  

2. **People Costs (+$418,633 / +51.2%)**  
   The department onboarded 12 new hires in Q1 to strengthen digital marketing, content creation, and analytics capabilities. Additionally, retention bonuses and higher-than-expected contractor utilization contributed to the variance. These investments align with our strategic focus on scaling internal expertise to reduce future reliance on external agencies.  

3. **Professional Services (+$165,158 / +41.7%)**  
   The variance reflects expanded partnerships with Edelman PR ($491,108) to support brand positioning and crisis management efforts. An unplanned engagement with a market research firm also contributed to the overspend, providing critical insights into customer behavior in key regions.  

4. **Technology (+$115,457 / +42.4%)**  
   Higher-than-anticipated costs for marketing automation tools, including Marketo ($498,801) and HubSpot ($426,952), drove this variance. These tools were upgraded to integrate advanced analytics and personalization features, enabling more effective campaign execution.
