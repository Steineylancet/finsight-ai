## Executive Summary  
The Human Resources (HR) department recorded total expenses of $715,471 in FY2025 Q1, exceeding the budget of $487,031 by $228,440 (+46.9%). This unfavorable variance was driven by higher-than-expected People Costs, Professional Services, and Technology expenditures, reflecting strategic investments in talent acquisition, employee development, and system upgrades. While these initiatives align with Crestwood Capital Group’s growth objectives, the variance underscores the need for enhanced cost forecasting and vendor management.  

---

## Expense Performance by Category  
- **People Costs:** Actuals of $355,657 exceeded the budget of $245,824 by $109,833 (+44.7%).  
- **Professional Services:** Actuals of $113,332 surpassed the budget of $71,195 by $42,136 (+59.2%).  
- **Technology:** Actuals of $106,664 exceeded the budget of $72,734 by $33,930 (+46.6%).  
- **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $71,603 were over budget by $22,373 (+45.4%).  
- **Facilities & Occupancy:** Actuals of $68,216 exceeded the budget of $48,048 by $20,168 (+42.0%).  

All categories posted unfavorable variances, with People Costs and Professional Services as the primary drivers.  

---

## Key Variances & Commentary  
1. **People Costs (+$109,833 / +44.7%)**  
   The variance reflects an aggressive hiring push to support business expansion, including 12 new hires across key functions. Additionally, onboarding costs, including training through Udemy Business ($44,350), exceeded expectations. Higher-than-budgeted healthcare premiums with Cigna Healthcare ($45,480) and retirement contributions with Principal Financial Group ($45,077) also contributed to the overage.  

2. **Professional Services (+$42,136 / +59.2%)**  
   The variance is tied to increased reliance on external consultants for talent acquisition and diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives. These services, while unbudgeted, are critical to meeting our workforce strategy goals.  

3. **Technology (+$33,930 / +46.6%)**  
   Investments in HR technology, including expanded licenses for Workday Inc. ($48,830) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($93,729), drove this variance. These tools enhance payroll processing, performance management, and compliance tracking but were implemented earlier than anticipated.  

4. **Travel & Expense (+$22,373 / +45.4%)**  
   The resumption of in-person recruiting events and leadership offsites contributed to higher travel costs. These activities were not fully accounted for during budget planning.  

5. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$20,168 / +42.0%)**  
   The variance reflects temporary office expansions to accommodate new hires and increased utility costs.  

---

## Key Vendors  
The top five vendors accounted for $277,466 in People Costs, with Cornerstone OnDemand ($93,729) and Workday Inc. ($48,830) as the largest contributors. These vendors are central to our HR technology ecosystem, supporting scalable growth.
