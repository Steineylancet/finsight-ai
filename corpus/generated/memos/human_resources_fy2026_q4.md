### **Executive Summary**  
As we approach FY2026 Q4, the Human Resources department is forecasted to close the quarter with total expenditures of $720,795, exceeding the budget of $711,564 by $9,231 (+1.3%). This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated costs in Professional Services, Technology, and Travel & Expense, partially offset by savings in Facilities & Occupancy. The forecast reflects ongoing investments in talent acquisition, HR technology enhancements, and employee engagement initiatives, which are critical to supporting Crestwood Capital Group’s strategic growth objectives.

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $358,257, slightly above budget by $1,966 (+0.6%). This increase reflects planned hiring activity, including onboarding costs for two senior-level hires and incremental merit adjustments to remain competitive in the labor market.  
2. **Technology**: Forecasted at $107,211, exceeding the budget by $1,000 (+0.9%). This variance is driven by the renewal of key HR software licenses and additional costs for implementing new analytics capabilities to enhance workforce planning.  
3. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $112,636, surpassing the budget by $6,668 (+6.3%). This increase is attributed to higher-than-anticipated consulting fees for ongoing DE&I initiatives and external support for leadership development programs.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $69,538, under budget by $2,231 (-3.1%). This reflects cost savings from reduced utilization of office space due to hybrid work arrangements.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $73,154, exceeding the budget by $1,829 (+2.6%). This increase is driven by planned travel for recruitment events and leadership offsite meetings in Q4.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Talent Acquisition**: Two key hires are planned for Q4, driving incremental People Costs.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals and enhancements to HR systems are expected to finalize in Q4, supporting Crestwood’s digital transformation goals.  
- **Employee Engagement Initiatives**: Continued focus on DE&I and leadership development is reflected in Professional Services spend.  
- **Seasonal Travel**: Recruitment and year-end strategic planning activities are expected to drive Travel & Expense costs higher.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Consulting Costs**: Any scope changes to DE&I or leadership programs could further increase Professional Services expenses.  
2. **Hiring Timelines**: Delays in planned hires may reduce People Costs but could impact operational capacity.  
3. **Technology Spend**: Potential cost overruns in system implementation should be closely monitored.  
4. **Travel Volatility**: Rising travel costs or unplanned events may further pressure the Travel & Expense category.
