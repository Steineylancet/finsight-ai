### **Executive Summary**  
The Data & Analytics department reported total expenses of $706,435 in FY2026 Q1, exceeding the budget of $479,746 by $226,689 (+47.3%). This variance was driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+48.5%), Technology investments (+47.3%), and Professional Services (+41.4%). While these variances reflect strategic initiatives and unanticipated growth in operational demands, they also underscore the need for tighter cost controls in future quarters.  

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs:** Actuals reached $425,888 against a budget of $286,752, resulting in an unfavorable variance of $139,136 (+48.5%).  
2. **Technology:** Expenses totaled $177,825, exceeding the budget of $120,711 by $57,114 (+47.3%).  
3. **Professional Services:** Actual spend was $67,952, surpassing the budget of $48,071 by $19,881 (+41.4%).  
4. **Travel & Expense:** Costs of $34,770 were $10,558 (+43.6%) above the budgeted $24,211.  

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
- **People Costs (+48.5%, +$139,136):**  
  The significant increase in People Costs is attributed to accelerated hiring for critical data science and analytics roles to support the company’s expansion into new markets. Additionally, vendor costs from Principal Financial Group ($601,160) and MetLife Insurance ($425,830) reflect higher-than-anticipated health and retirement benefits associated with onboarding new employees. Recruitment efforts through Indeed.com and LinkedIn Talent Solutions also contributed to the variance, with combined costs of $750,460.  

- **Technology (+47.3%, +$57,114):**  
  The department invested heavily in upgrading cloud infrastructure and analytics platforms to enhance data processing capabilities. These investments were necessary to support the increased volume of data generated by new business lines. However, the timing of these expenses was earlier than anticipated, contributing to the unfavorable variance.  

- **Professional Services (+41.4%, +$19,881):**  
  The department engaged external consultants to expedite the implementation of machine learning models for predictive analytics. While this initiative aligns with strategic goals, the upfront costs exceeded initial estimates.  

- **Travel & Expense (+43.6%, +$10,558):**  
  Increased travel was driven by cross-functional collaboration with regional teams, particularly for training sessions on newly implemented analytics tools. While these costs were necessary to ensure smooth adoption, they exceeded budgeted levels due to higher-than-expected airfare and accommodation rates.  

### **Key Vendors**  
The top five vendors for Q1 were all related to People Costs, with Principal Financial Group ($601,160) and MetLife Insurance ($425,830) being the largest contributors. These reflect the department’s focus on attracting and retaining top talent through competitive benefits packages. Recruitment platforms like Indeed.com ($418,454) and LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($332,006) also played a pivotal role in scaling the team.
