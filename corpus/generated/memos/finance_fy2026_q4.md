## Executive Summary  
As we approach FY2026 Q4, the Finance department is forecasted to end the quarter with total expenses of $978,408, exceeding the budget of $969,212 by $9,196 (+0.9%). This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs and Professional Services, partially offset by savings in Facilities & Occupancy and Travel & Expense. The forecast reflects anticipated investments in strategic initiatives, balanced by cost management efforts in discretionary categories.  

---

## Planned Spend by Category  
- **People Costs**: Forecasted at $539,128, exceeding budget by $5,127 (+1.0%). This increase reflects the onboarding of two planned hires in Q4 to support critical reporting and compliance functions.  
- **Professional Services**: Projected at $205,063, surpassing budget by $10,749 (+5.5%). The variance is driven by continued reliance on external consultants for the ERP system upgrade, which is entering its final implementation phase.  
- **Technology**: Expected at $115,737, slightly over budget by $1,646 (+1.4%). This includes incremental costs related to software license renewals and cloud storage expansion to accommodate growing data needs.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $71,654, under budget by $7,355 (-9.3%). Savings are attributed to renegotiated office lease terms and reduced utility expenses as hybrid work arrangements persist.  
- **Travel & Expense**: Anticipated at $46,826, below budget by $970 (-2.0%). Lower-than-expected travel activity for industry conferences and client meetings has contributed to this modest reduction.  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  
- **Strategic Hiring**: The forecast assumes the successful onboarding of two new team members in Q4, contributing to the slight increase in People Costs.  
- **ERP Implementation**: Professional Services expenses reflect ongoing consultant support for the ERP system upgrade, which is critical to enhancing operational efficiency in FY2027.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals for key software licenses and expanded cloud storage are necessary to support data-driven decision-making and compliance requirements.  
- **Facilities Optimization**: Continued savings from hybrid work arrangements and renegotiated lease terms are expected to persist through Q4.  
- **Seasonal Trends**: Travel & Expense forecasts account for typical Q4 seasonality, with reduced travel activity compared to earlier quarters.  

---

3. **Technology Cost Escalation**: Potential increases in software licensing fees or unexpected IT infrastructure needs could pressure the Technology budget.
