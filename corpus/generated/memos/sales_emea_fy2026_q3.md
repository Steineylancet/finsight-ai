### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q3, the Sales - EMEA department is forecasted to exceed its budget by $1.54M (+4.6%), with total projected spend of $35.14M against a budget of $33.60M. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated Renewals (+$865K, +5.1%) and New Business (+$302K, +4.1%) activity, alongside incremental increases in Professional Services (+$240K, +6.3%). These upward adjustments reflect a strong pipeline of client engagements, an increase in renewal volume, and expanded project scopes. While most categories are forecasted to remain within a reasonable variance range, Facilities & Occupancy (+8.2%) and Professional Services (+6.3%) warrant close monitoring.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **Renewals ($17.97M, +5.1%)**: Renewals represent the largest category variance, driven by a higher-than-expected volume of contract renewals in Q3. This reflects strong customer retention efforts and favorable market conditions.  
- **New Business ($7.63M, +4.1%)**: Increased spend is linked to planned investments in sales enablement tools and targeted campaigns to support new client acquisition.  
- **Professional Services ($4.03M, +6.3%)**: Higher forecasted costs are attributed to expanded project scopes and increased reliance on external consultants to support complex implementations.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy ($316K, +8.2%)**: This variance reflects temporary office expansions in key markets to accommodate planned headcount growth.  
- **Travel & Expense ($590K, -1.1%)**: Travel costs are forecasted slightly below budget due to a strategic shift towards virtual engagements in certain regions.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
1. **Renewal Pipeline Strength**: Q3 is a seasonally high renewal period, with several large contracts expected to close, driving both revenue and associated costs.  
2. **New Business Growth**: Increased spend in sales enablement and marketing reflects a focus on capturing market share in emerging EMEA markets.  
3. **Professional Services Demand**: Several in-flight client projects require specialized expertise, leading to higher-than-budgeted consulting and professional services costs.  
4. **Planned Hiring**: Modest headcount growth in sales and account management teams is factored into People Costs (+1.9%).  

---
