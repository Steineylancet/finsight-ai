## Executive Summary  

For FY2026 Q2, the Customer Success – EMEA department is forecasted to spend $1,224,051, a slight increase of $824 (+0.1%) compared to the budget of $1,223,227. This variance reflects minor adjustments across multiple expense categories, with notable overspend in Professional Services (+6.5%) offset by savings in Travel & Expense (-1.4%) and Technology (-0.8%). The forecast aligns closely with the budget, indicating disciplined cost management while accommodating strategic initiatives.  

---

## Planned Spend by Category  

1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $675,565, marginally above budget (+$652, +0.1%). This reflects planned headcount increases to support customer retention initiatives and onboarding for new enterprise accounts in the EMEA region.  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Expected to come in at $180,845, under budget by $2,576 (-1.4%). Reduced travel is anticipated due to a shift toward virtual customer workshops and a focus on regional, rather than cross-border, travel.  
3. **Technology**: Forecasted at $181,483, slightly below budget (-$1,473, -0.8%). This reflects cost efficiencies from renegotiated software licenses and delayed implementation of certain tools.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Projected at $122,626, modestly over budget (+$355, +0.3%) due to inflationary adjustments in office lease agreements.  
5. **Professional Services**: Anticipated at $63,532, exceeding budget by $3,866 (+6.5%). This increase is driven by expanded use of external consultants for customer success process optimization and training programs.  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  

- **Headcount Growth**: The forecast assumes the addition of three Customer Success Managers during Q2 to support a growing customer base in key EMEA markets.  
- **Travel Optimization**: A continued emphasis on virtual engagements is expected to reduce travel-related costs while maintaining customer engagement levels.  
- **Technology Savings**: Cost reductions stem from renegotiated contracts and deferred rollouts for non-critical tools.  
- **Professional Services Investment**: Higher spend in this category reflects a strategic focus on improving customer satisfaction and retention through external expertise.  

---

## Risks & Watch Items  

1. **Economic Conditions**: Macroeconomic uncertainty in the EMEA region could impact customer expansion plans, potentially affecting resource allocation and travel needs.  
2. **Consultant Dependency**: Increased reliance on external consultants for process improvements may introduce risks if deliverables are delayed or fail to meet expectations.  
3. **Technology Timelines**: Delayed implementation of certain tools could impact team productivity or customer experience if alternative solutions are not identified.  
4. **Inflationary Pressures**: Rising costs in facilities and travel could exceed current forecasts if inflation accelerates
