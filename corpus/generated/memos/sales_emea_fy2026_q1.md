### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q1, the EMEA Sales department reported total expenses of $31.37M, exceeding the budget of $28.57M by $2.80M (+9.8%). While revenue growth initiatives have driven increased activity, several expense categories experienced significant budget overruns, particularly People Costs (+43.8%), Renewals (+7.0%), and Professional Services (+10.2%). These variances reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition, customer retention, and infrastructure to support long-term growth in the region. However, the magnitude of these overruns requires closer scrutiny to ensure cost discipline in future quarters.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
- **Renewals:** Actuals of $15.20M exceeded the $14.20M budget by $1.00M (+7.0%).  
- **People Costs:** Actuals of $2.01M exceeded the $1.40M budget by $0.61M (+43.8%).  
- **Professional Services:** Actuals of $3.56M exceeded the $3.23M budget by $0.33M (+10.2%).  
- **New Business:** Actuals of $6.46M were slightly above the $6.22M budget by $0.24M (+3.9%).  
- **Travel & Expense (T&E):** Actuals of $0.75M exceeded the $0.56M budget by $0.19M (+34.4%).  
- **Technology:** Actuals of $0.41M exceeded the $0.27M budget by $0.14M (+50.5%).  
- **Marketing:** Actuals of $0.39M exceeded the $0.27M budget by $0.12M (+45.6%).

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+43.8%, $0.61M over budget):**  
   This variance reflects an accelerated hiring push to meet regional growth targets, particularly in high-demand markets like Germany and the UAE. Top vendors such as Udemy Business ($773K) and LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($551K) contributed to increased costs for onboarding, training, and recruitment.  

2. **Renewals (+7.0%, $1.00M over budget):**  
   Higher-than-expected costs were driven by elevated customer retention efforts, including expanded account management support and incentives for key clients. This investment aligns with our strategy to stabilize recurring revenue streams in a competitive market.  

3. **Professional Services (+10.2%, $0.33M over budget):**
