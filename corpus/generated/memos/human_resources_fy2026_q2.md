## Executive Summary  
The Human Resources (HR) department is projected to close FY2026 Q2 with total expenditures of $619,709, reflecting a modest $5,195 (+0.8%) variance against the approved budget of $614,514. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated technology investments, partially offset by savings in professional services and facilities costs. The forecast aligns with HR's strategic priorities, including talent acquisition, employee engagement initiatives, and ongoing technology upgrades to support workforce optimization.  

---

## Planned Spend by Category  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $310,991, this category is expected to exceed budget by $1,687 (+0.5%). The slight increase is attributed to planned mid-quarter hires to support growth in key business functions and adjustments to employee benefit costs.  
2. **Technology**: Projected to reach $98,032, exceeding budget by $6,807 (+7.5%). This increase is driven by the accelerated rollout of an upgraded HRIS platform and additional software licenses to support expanded headcount.  
3. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $89,035, reflecting a $1,235 (-1.4%) underspend. This variance is due to renegotiated vendor contracts for recruitment services and deferred consulting engagements.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Anticipated to come in at $60,408, under budget by $2,185 (-3.5%). Savings stem from reduced office space utilization as hybrid work policies remain in effect.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Projected at $61,243, marginally above budget by $120 (+0.2%). This reflects anticipated travel for leadership development programs and campus recruitment events.  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  
- **Talent Acquisition**: The forecast assumes the onboarding of five new hires in Q2 to support business growth, contributing to the slight increase in people costs.  
- **Technology Investments**: The HRIS system upgrade, initially planned for Q3, has been expedited to Q2 to address scalability needs. Additional licenses for collaboration tools are also included.  
- **Seasonal Trends**: Recruitment activity is expected to peak in Q2, aligning with the spring hiring cycle, driving incremental travel and technology expenses.  

---

## Risks & Watch Items  
- **Hiring Delays**: Any delays in planned hiring could result in underspend in people costs but may impact operational capacity.  
- **Technology Implementation**: The accelerated HRIS rollout carries execution risk, including potential cost overruns or delays.  
- **Vendor Dependencies**: Savings in professional services are contingent on timely delivery from external partners.  

The HR team remains focused on managing these risks while delivering on strategic priorities. We will continue to monitor spending trends and provide updates as needed.
