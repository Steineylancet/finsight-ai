### **Executive Summary**  
The FY2026 Q4 forecast for the EMEA Sales department projects total spending of $44.0M, exceeding the budget of $43.1M by $876.6K (+2.0%). The primary drivers of this variance are higher-than-anticipated revenue-generating activities, including Renewals (+$340.4K, +1.6%), New Business (+$288.1K, +2.8%), and Consulting (+$130.8K, +4.3%). These increases reflect strategic investments in customer retention, growth initiatives, and external expertise to support key projects. While most categories are tracking slightly above budget, Travel & Expense (-$18.3K, -2.5%) and Marketing (-$0.7K, -0.2%) are forecasted to come in below budget, reflecting cost discipline in discretionary areas.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **Renewals ($21.8M, +1.6%)**: The forecast reflects an uptick in renewal activity, supported by a strong pipeline of major contracts set to close in Q4.  
- **New Business ($10.5M, +2.8%)**: Increased investment is driven by planned expansion into high-growth markets and additional headcount for business development.  
- **Professional Services ($4.8M, +0.9%)**: Spending aligns with ongoing client engagements and implementation projects.  
- **Consulting ($3.2M, +4.3%)**: Higher-than-budgeted spend reflects the onboarding of external consultants to support strategic initiatives, including CRM optimization and market entry studies.  
- **People Costs ($1.9M, +4.3%)**: The variance is attributed to planned hires in sales and account management roles, as well as merit-based compensation adjustments.  
- **Travel & Expense ($717.8K, -2.5%)**: Reduced travel spend reflects a shift to virtual client meetings and internal cost control measures.  
- **Marketing ($363.9K, -0.2%)**: Marketing spend is tracking close to budget, with a focus on digital campaigns targeting Q4 deal acceleration.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy ($367.8K, +2.6%)**: Slightly higher costs are linked to increased office utilization in key markets.  
- **Technology ($362.5K, +1.5%)**: Incremental investments are tied to software upgrades and tools to enhance sales productivity.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
The forecast assumes continued momentum in renewal and new business pipelines, supported by seasonally strong Q4 activity. Planned investments in sales headcount and consulting services are expected to drive topline growth. Cost efficiencies in travel and marketing are anticipated to partially offset higher spending in other areas.
