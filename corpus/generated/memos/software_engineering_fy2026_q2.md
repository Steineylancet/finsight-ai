## Executive Summary  
The Software Engineering department’s FY2026 Q2 forecast projects total expenditures of $8,820,660, exceeding the budget of $8,655,135 by $165,525 (+1.9%). Key cost drivers include increased People Costs due to planned headcount growth and modest overages in Technology and Travel & Expense categories. These increases are partially offset by minor savings in Professional Services. The overall variance remains within an acceptable range, reflecting disciplined cost management amid strategic investments in talent and technology to support ongoing initiatives.

---

## Planned Spend by Category  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $5,773,015, a $130,774 (+2.3%) increase over budget. This reflects planned onboarding of additional software engineers and product managers to support high-priority development projects and platform enhancements.  
2. **Technology**: Forecasted at $1,738,999, exceeding budget by $22,261 (+1.3%). This variance is driven by anticipated mid-quarter renewals of critical software licenses and incremental spending on cloud infrastructure to scale ongoing projects.  
3. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $691,317, slightly below budget by $6,411 (-0.9%). This reflects reduced reliance on external consultants as internal teams take on more project deliverables.  
4. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $444,040, exceeding budget by $13,423 (+3.1%). The increase is attributed to planned travel for cross-functional team collaboration and attendance at key industry conferences.  
5. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $173,289, a $5,478 (+3.3%) increase over budget due to higher-than-expected office utility costs and minor facility upgrades to accommodate new hires.

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  
- **Headcount Growth**: The forecast assumes 15 new hires in Q2, aligned with strategic workforce expansion to meet project demands.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals of software licenses and incremental cloud infrastructure spending are expected to maintain operational efficiency and scalability.  
- **Seasonal Travel**: Increased travel activity is anticipated as teams participate in mid-year planning sessions and industry events.  
- **Facilities Costs**: Slight upticks in occupancy costs are expected as office space utilization increases with new hires.

---

## Risks & Watch Items  
- **Hiring Delays**: Any delays in onboarding planned hires could reduce People Costs but may impact project timelines.  
- **Technology Cost Volatility**: Unexpected price increases in software renewals or cloud services could further inflate Technology expenses.  
- **Travel Disruptions**: External factors such as travel restrictions or cost inflation could affect Travel & Expense forecasts.  
- **Facilities Utilization**: Higher-than-anticipated occupancy costs could arise if hiring outpaces current office capacity.
