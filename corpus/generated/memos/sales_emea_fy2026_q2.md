### **Executive Summary**  
The FY2026 Q2 forecast for the EMEA Sales department projects total expenditures of $41.1M, exceeding the budget of $39.3M by $1.75M (+4.5%). This variance is primarily driven by increased investments in renewals, new business acquisition, and professional services, reflecting a strategic focus on revenue growth and customer retention. While most expense categories are forecasted to exceed budget, facilities costs are expected to come in below plan (-$13.4K, -4.2%). This forecast aligns with Crestwood Capital Group’s broader growth objectives in the EMEA region, supported by ongoing initiatives to expand market share and enhance service delivery.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **Renewals ($20.5M, +4.3% vs. budget):** The largest category increase reflects a robust pipeline of high-value contract renewals in Q2, supported by expanded account management resources.  
- **New Business ($9.8M, +2.7%):** Incremental spend is tied to targeted campaigns in key growth markets and increased investment in sales enablement tools.  
- **Professional Services ($4.4M, +7.3%):** Higher projected spend stems from ongoing client implementation projects and additional consulting engagements to support complex deals.  
- **Consulting ($3.1M, +8.1%):** This increase reflects anticipated reliance on external expertise for strategic initiatives, including market expansion and pricing optimization.  
- **People Costs ($1.7M, +7.4%):** The variance is attributed to planned headcount growth, including new hires in sales and account management roles to support regional demand.  
- **Travel & Expense ($629.5K, +0.4%):** Minimal increase reflects steady travel activity for client meetings and industry events.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy ($309K, -4.2%):** Savings are expected due to renegotiated lease terms for regional offices.  
- **Technology ($329.5K, +5.3%):** Incremental spend is tied to software upgrades and enhanced CRM capabilities.  
- **Marketing ($316.8K, +1.2%):** Increased investment in digital campaigns and event sponsorships is driving this modest variance.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
The forecast assumes continued strong demand for renewals and new business in EMEA, supported by favorable economic conditions and Crestwood’s competitive positioning. Planned headcount additions are expected to drive higher people costs, while professional services and consulting spend will support critical client-facing initiatives. Technology investments will enhance operational efficiency and enable sales teams to capitalize on growth opportunities.
