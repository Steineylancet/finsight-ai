### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q4, Finance Operations is forecasting total expenses of $2,236,148, which represents a $92,964 (+4.3%) increase over the budget of $2,143,184. The primary drivers of this variance are higher-than-anticipated People Costs and incremental Technology investments, partially offset by savings in Facilities & Occupancy and Travel & Expense. This forecast reflects strategic decisions to support ongoing initiatives, including technology upgrades and resource expansion, while maintaining cost discipline in discretionary areas.

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $1,252,488, exceeding budget by $86,296 (+7.4%). This increase reflects planned hiring to support critical finance transformation initiatives, including two senior analysts and one project manager, as well as higher-than-expected merit adjustments and benefits costs.  
   
2. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $438,785, slightly below budget by $1,243 (-0.3%). This reflects a stable outlook for consulting engagements, with no significant changes to planned advisory support for compliance and process optimization projects.  

3. **Technology**: Forecasted at $272,094, exceeding budget by $15,695 (+6.1%). The variance is driven by mid-quarter renewals for key software licenses and incremental investments in financial reporting tools to enhance automation and analytics capabilities.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $166,630, under budget by $6,473 (-3.7%). Savings are attributed to continued optimization of office space utilization and renegotiation of lease terms.  

5. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $106,151, under budget by $1,312 (-1.2%). This reflects disciplined travel planning, with most meetings and training sessions conducted virtually.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Headcount Growth**: The forecast assumes three planned hires in Q4 to meet increasing operational demands and support strategic projects.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals for financial systems and expanded use of analytics platforms are expected to drive efficiencies in reporting and forecasting processes.  
- **Seasonal Trends**: Q4 typically sees lower travel activity and reduced facilities costs due to holiday schedules, contributing to savings in these categories.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
- **People Costs**: The forecast assumes timely onboarding of new hires; delays could impact project timelines and resource availability.  
- **Technology Spend**: Vendor pricing fluctuations or unanticipated software needs could increase costs.  
- **Professional Services Utilization**: While stable, any scope expansion for consulting engagements could create budget pressure.  

We will continue to monitor these areas closely and provide updates as needed. Proactive cost management and alignment with strategic priorities remain our focus as we close out FY2026.
