## **Executive Summary**  
The Data & Analytics department is forecasted to close FY2026 Q4 with total expenditures of $675,431, exceeding the budget by $24,669 (+3.8%). This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs, offset partially by minor underspending in Technology and Professional Services. The forecast reflects strategic investments in talent and ongoing projects critical to the company’s data-driven initiatives.  

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **People Costs:** Forecasted at $419,791, exceeding budget by $28,148 (+7.2%). This increase is attributed to planned headcount growth, including the onboarding of two senior data engineers in December, and higher-than-expected contractor utilization to support the rollout of the advanced analytics platform.  
- **Technology:** Forecasted at $160,223, slightly under budget by $1,482 (-0.9%). Savings stem from favorable vendor pricing on a key software renewal negotiated in Q3.  
- **Professional Services:** Forecasted at $63,387, undershooting budget by $2,314 (-3.5%). Lower-than-expected reliance on external consultants for the data migration project has contributed to this variance.  
- **Travel & Expense:** Forecasted at $32,030, marginally over budget by $316 (+1.0%). This reflects increased travel for team members attending the Data Innovation Summit in late November.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
1. **Talent Investments:** The forecast assumes the planned hiring of two senior data engineers by mid-December to support critical deliverables for the advanced analytics platform. Additionally, contractor hours are expected to remain elevated through quarter-end.  
2. **Technology Optimization:** The slight underspend in Technology reflects cost efficiencies achieved through proactive vendor negotiations, which are expected to continue into FY2027.  
3. **Project Timelines:** The data migration project is progressing ahead of schedule, reducing reliance on external consultants in Q4. However, internal resource allocation remains a key focus.  
4. **Seasonal Travel:** Travel expenses are forecasted to increase slightly in November due to participation in industry events, aligning with the department’s strategic objectives for knowledge sharing and innovation.  

---

## **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Hiring Delays:** Any delays in onboarding the planned hires could impact the department’s ability to meet Q4 project milestones, potentially requiring additional contractor support.  
2. **Contractor Costs:** While contractor utilization is expected to taper post-project launch, any scope changes or delays could extend their engagement, further pressuring People Costs.  
3. **Technology Renewals:** Although current savings are reflected in the forecast, upcoming software renewals in early FY2027 may introduce cost pressures if vendor terms shift.  
4. **Travel Volatility:** Unanticipated travel needs or higher-than-expected participation in industry events could marginally increase Travel & Expense costs
