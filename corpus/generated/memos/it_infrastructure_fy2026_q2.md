### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q2, the IT Infrastructure department is forecasted to spend $3,975,660, exceeding the budget of $3,820,999 by $154,661 (+4.0%). The variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated costs in Technology (+6.0%) and People Costs (+3.9%), partially offset by a modest reduction in Professional Services (-1.8%). This forecast reflects ongoing investments in critical infrastructure upgrades, planned headcount growth, and operational adjustments to support Crestwood Capital Group’s strategic initiatives.

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **Technology**: Forecasted at $1,801,431, exceeding the budget by $101,237 (+6.0%). This increase is attributed to higher-than-expected costs for software license renewals, cloud storage expansion to support increased data usage, and the procurement of additional hardware for upcoming system upgrades.  
2. **People Costs**: Projected at $1,405,026, up $52,966 (+3.9%) from the budget, reflecting planned hiring of three additional infrastructure engineers to support new projects and market-driven salary adjustments.  
3. **Professional Services**: Anticipated to come in slightly below budget at $453,296 (-1.8%), as certain consulting engagements have been scaled back or deferred to later quarters.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Expected to total $199,378, exceeding budget by $7,736 (+4.0%), due to incremental costs associated with expanding server room capacity.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $116,529, modestly over budget by $968 (+0.8%), reflecting travel related to vendor negotiations and on-site project management for key initiatives.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Strategic Investments**: The forecast incorporates continued investment in cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity enhancements to support Crestwood’s digital transformation goals.  
- **Headcount Growth**: The addition of three FTEs is planned to address resource gaps and ensure timely delivery of high-priority projects.  
- **Seasonality**: Q2 typically sees higher technology-related spend due to mid-year license renewals and hardware procurement cycles.  
- **Cost Management**: Professional Services savings reflect a deliberate effort to reprioritize external consulting engagements.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Vendor Pricing Volatility**: Potential fluctuations in software and hardware pricing could further impact Technology costs.  
2. **Hiring Delays**: Any delays in onboarding planned hires could affect People Costs and project timelines.  
3. **Project Scope Creep**: Unanticipated changes to in-flight projects could lead to additional Professional Services or Technology expenditures.  
4. **Facilities Constraints**: Expansion of server room capacity may require additional unbudgeted investments if current estimates prove insufficient.
