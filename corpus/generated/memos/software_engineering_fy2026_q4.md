## **Executive Summary**  
As we approach FY2026 Q4, the Software Engineering department is forecasted to close the quarter with total expenses of $9.90M, reflecting a modest 0.8% increase ($79.3K) over the approved budget of $9.83M. The variance is primarily driven by incremental investments in technology and travel, partially offset by savings in professional services. These adjustments align with strategic priorities such as scaling development capacity, supporting key technology initiatives, and enabling in-person collaboration. Overall, the department remains on track to deliver within an acceptable range of the budget, with no material risks to financial performance currently identified.

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $6.40M, a slight 0.3% ($17K) over budget. This reflects planned hiring of three additional software engineers to support the accelerated delivery of the Q4 product roadmap.  
2. **Technology**: Forecasted at $2.01M, exceeding budget by 2.7% ($53K), driven by anticipated renewals of key cloud infrastructure contracts and incremental licensing costs for development tools.  
3. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $783K, under budget by 1.9% ($15K). Reduced reliance on external consultants for ongoing projects has contributed to this favorable variance.  
4. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $508K, exceeding budget by 4.3% ($21K). This increase reflects planned team offsites and participation in industry conferences to foster collaboration and innovation.  
5. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $203K, slightly over budget by 1.7% ($3K) due to minor adjustments in shared office space allocations.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Hiring & Capacity Expansion**: The forecast assumes three additional hires in Q4, aligned with the department’s strategic focus on scaling engineering capacity to meet growing demand.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals of cloud infrastructure and software licenses are expected to drive higher technology spend, ensuring uninterrupted support for development and testing environments.  
- **Travel Resumption**: Increased travel reflects a strategic emphasis on in-person collaboration, particularly for cross-functional planning and knowledge-sharing events.  
- **Professional Services Optimization**: Reduced reliance on external consultants reflects the successful onboarding of internal resources and improved project execution efficiency.  

---

## **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Hiring Delays**: Any delays in onboarding planned hires could impact project timelines and reduce forecasted people costs.  
2. **Contract Renewals**: Final pricing for cloud and software renewals is still under negotiation, which could result in slight deviations from the forecast.  
3. **Travel Volatility**: Travel forecasts assume no significant disruptions; however, unforeseen changes in travel policies or external factors could impact spend.
