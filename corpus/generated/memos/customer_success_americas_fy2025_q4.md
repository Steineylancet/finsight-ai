### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **Professional Services (-$124,681, -100%)**  
   The department deferred planned consulting engagements tied to client onboarding optimization and retention strategy development. These initiatives were postponed due to the organizational realignment and are expected to resume in FY2026.

2. **Facilities & Occupancy (-$250,049, -100%)**  
   The department’s transition to a fully remote operating model eliminated the need for physical office space in Q4. This change aligns with broader company efforts to reduce fixed costs and increase operational flexibility.

3. **Travel & Expense (-$377,505, -100%)**  
   All travel was suspended during the quarter as part of the cost-containment strategy. Additionally, the shift to virtual client engagements further reduced the need for travel-related expenditures.

4. **Technology (-$378,059, -100%)**  
   Planned investments in customer success platforms and analytics tools were deferred pending a company-wide review of technology priorities. These expenditures will likely be revisited in FY2026.

5. **People Costs (-$1,382,479, -100%)**  
   The department experienced a temporary reduction in headcount due to the realignment. Affected roles are expected to be restructured and partially backfilled in the coming quarters to meet operational demands.
