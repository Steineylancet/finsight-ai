### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q2, the Finance department’s total forecasted spend is $849,037, representing a modest increase of $6,170 (+0.7%) compared to the budget of $842,866. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs (+$11,998 or +2.6%), partially offset by reductions in Professional Services (-$4,173 or -2.5%) and Technology (-$1,940 or -1.9%). The forecast reflects careful alignment with strategic priorities, including planned headcount growth, technology optimization, and ongoing cost containment efforts.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $474,683, exceeding the budget by $11,998 (+2.6%). This increase reflects planned hiring of two additional team members in Q2 to support the company’s strategic initiatives, as well as higher-than-expected benefit costs due to market-driven adjustments.  
2. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $165,456, undershooting the budget by $4,173 (-2.5%). This reduction is attributed to the renegotiation of consulting contracts and the deferral of certain advisory projects to later quarters.  
3. **Technology**: Forecasted at $99,307, slightly below budget by $1,940 (-1.9%). Savings stem from the early renewal of a key software license at a discounted rate, partially offset by incremental investments in data analytics tools.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $67,189, closely aligned with the budget (-$339 or -0.5%). No significant changes are anticipated in this category.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $42,402, exceeding the budget by $624 (+1.5%). This reflects increased travel activity for leadership participation in industry conferences and client-facing engagements.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Headcount Expansion**: Two planned hires will drive incremental People Costs, supporting critical finance transformation initiatives.  
- **Contract Efficiencies**: Savings in Professional Services and Technology reflect proactive vendor negotiations and timing adjustments for non-urgent projects.  
- **Seasonal Travel Uptick**: Q2 typically experiences higher travel volumes due to mid-year strategic planning sessions and external stakeholder meetings.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Labor Market Pressures**: Continued wage inflation or challenges in securing qualified talent could further elevate People Costs.  
2. **Vendor Dependencies**: Delays in vendor deliverables or unexpected cost escalations could impact Professional Services and Technology spend.  
3. **Travel Volatility**: Fluctuations in airfare and lodging rates may result in higher-than-forecasted Travel & Expense costs.
