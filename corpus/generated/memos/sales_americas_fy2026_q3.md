## **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q3, the Sales - Americas team is forecasted to exceed the budget by $1.71M (+3.2%), with total spend projected at $55.14M against a budget of $53.43M. Key drivers include higher-than-expected Renewals (+$903K, +3.3%) and New Business (+$482K, +4.1%), reflecting strong sales momentum and increased investment in revenue-generating activities. While most categories are tracking above budget, Travel & Expense (-$3K, -0.4%) and Marketing (-$7K, -1.5%) are forecasted slightly below plan, reflecting cost efficiencies.  

This outlook underscores the team’s commitment to driving growth while maintaining disciplined expense management.  

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **Renewals ($28.24M, +3.3% vs. budget):** Renewals are forecasted to outperform due to a robust pipeline of high-value contracts set to close in Q3.  
2. **New Business ($12.22M, +4.1% vs. budget):** Increased investment in sales enablement and lead generation is expected to drive higher deal volumes.  
3. **Professional Services ($6.13M, +2.5% vs. budget):** Incremental spend reflects ongoing client implementations and expanded service engagements.  
4. **Consulting ($3.96M, +3.9% vs. budget):** Additional consulting support is planned to accelerate go-to-market strategies and optimize sales processes.  
5. **People Costs ($2.32M, +1.3% vs. budget):** The forecast includes planned headcount growth to support the expanding sales pipeline.  
6. **Travel & Expense ($893K, -0.4% vs. budget):** Slightly reduced travel activity is anticipated due to virtual engagements for select client meetings.  
7. **Marketing ($450K, -1.5% vs. budget):** Lower spend reflects timing adjustments for campaigns originally planned for Q3.  
8. **Facilities & Occupancy ($450K, -0.8% vs. budget):** Minimal variance driven by operational efficiencies.  
9. **Technology ($462K, +2.1% vs. budget):** Increased investment in sales tools and CRM enhancements to support productivity.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Renewals and New Business:** Strong sales execution and favorable market conditions are expected to drive higher-than-budgeted revenue.  
- **Professional Services & Consulting:** Increased client demand and strategic initiatives necessitate additional support.  
- **People Costs:** Planned hiring of sales and support staff aligns with growth objectives.  
- **Technology:** Investments in tools to enhance sales efficiency remain a priority.
