## **Executive Summary**  
The IT Infrastructure department reported total expenses of $4,441,230 in FY2025 Q1, exceeding the budget of $3,080,986 by $1,360,244 (+44.1%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$549,701, +50.8%) and Technology expenses (+$549,630, +39.8%). These overages reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition and critical technology upgrades to support Crestwood Capital Group’s expanding operational needs. While these costs are significant, they align with our long-term growth initiatives, including enhanced cloud capabilities, data analytics infrastructure, and automation tools.

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  

### **People Costs (+$549,701, +50.8%)**  
The variance is attributed to accelerated hiring for key technical roles, including cloud engineers and cybersecurity specialists, to support the company’s digital transformation initiatives. Additionally, contractor utilization increased to meet aggressive project timelines for the Snowflake data platform migration and ServiceNow automation enhancements.  

### **Technology (+$549,630, +39.8%)**  
Higher-than-budgeted technology costs reflect strategic investments in cloud infrastructure and analytics tools. Key drivers include increased usage of Amazon Web Services (AWS) (+$506,603) to support scalable data storage and processing needs, and Tableau Software (+$412,068) for advanced data visualization capabilities. Additionally, Apple Inc. expenses (+$513,832) were driven by hardware refreshes for IT staff and end-user devices.  

### **Professional Services (+$152,519, +41.0%)**  
The variance stems from consulting engagements to support the Snowflake migration and ServiceNow workflow optimization. These services were critical to ensure a seamless transition and to mitigate operational risks during implementation.  

### **Facilities & Occupancy (+$68,085, +44.0%)**  
The overage reflects temporary co-working space leases to accommodate the expanded IT team, as well as higher utility costs associated with increased data center activity.  

### **Travel & Expense (+$40,308, +43.3%)**  
Higher travel costs were incurred for on-site vendor negotiations, training sessions for new tools, and team collaboration workshops at regional hubs.
