## **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q2, the Executive Department’s total forecasted spend is $2,257,203, reflecting a modest increase of $19,294 (+0.9%) compared to the approved budget of $2,237,909. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated costs in Facilities & Occupancy (+4.6%) and Insurance & Risk (+6.4%), partially offset by savings in Travel & Expense (-2.3%). While the overall variance is minimal, increased costs in key categories warrant close monitoring to ensure alignment with strategic priorities.

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $1,352,553 (+0.6% vs. budget), this slight increase reflects planned mid-quarter onboarding of senior hires to support strategic initiatives.  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $392,122 (-2.3%), this reduction assumes continued optimization of travel policies and a shift toward virtual engagements for key meetings.  
3. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $226,481 (+2.9%), the variance reflects incremental consulting support for ongoing leadership development programs and strategic planning efforts.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $165,080 (+4.6%), driven by anticipated increases in office maintenance and utility costs, as well as minor upgrades to executive office spaces.  
5. **Insurance & Risk**: Forecasted at $120,967 (+6.4%), reflecting higher premiums tied to recent policy renewals and expanded coverage for executive-level risk management.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Strategic Hiring**: Incremental People Costs are tied to the planned addition of two senior executives in Q2 to support growth initiatives.  
- **Travel Optimization**: Reduced Travel & Expense reflects a continued focus on cost discipline, leveraging virtual platforms for non-critical engagements.  
- **Consulting Engagements**: Professional Services costs are expected to rise due to expanded use of external consultants for leadership training and strategic initiatives.  
- **Facilities Maintenance**: Facilities & Occupancy increases are driven by routine office upgrades and higher utility rates during the summer months.  
- **Insurance Renewals**: The increase in Insurance & Risk costs reflects higher premiums negotiated during annual policy renewals, ensuring adequate coverage for executive operations.  

---

1. **Macroeconomic Conditions**: Rising inflation or unexpected economic shifts could further pressure Facilities & Occupancy and Insurance costs.

3. **Consulting Scope Creep**: Professional Services costs could escalate if project scopes are expanded beyond initial estimates.
