## **Executive Summary**  
The Data & Analytics (D&A) department reported total expenses of $711,831 in FY2025 Q3, exceeding the budget of $488,133 by $223,699 (+45.8%). Variances were driven by higher-than-expected people costs, increased technology investments, and elevated professional services spend. While these overages reflect strategic initiatives to scale data-driven capabilities, cost management will require closer monitoring in future quarters to align with budgetary expectations.  

---

## **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs:** Actuals of $418,513 exceeded the budget of $294,342 by $124,170 (+42.2%).  
2. **Technology:** Actuals of $188,895 exceeded the budget of $119,777 by $69,118 (+57.7%).  
3. **Professional Services:** Actuals of $70,096 exceeded the budget of $49,107 by $20,989 (+42.7%).  
4. **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $34,328 exceeded the budget of $24,906 by $9,422 (+37.8%).  

All categories reported unfavorable variances, with people costs and technology accounting for 86.5% of the total overspend.  

---

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
### **People Costs (+$124,170 | +42.2%)**  
The increase in people costs reflects a combination of headcount expansion and investments in employee development. The department onboarded three senior data engineers and two analysts in Q3 to support the rollout of the Predictive Analytics Platform (PAP), a key FY2025 initiative. Additionally, training programs through Udemy Business ($404,290) and LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($297,666) were scaled up to upskill existing staff in machine learning and data visualization. These investments are expected to enhance long-term productivity but have temporarily inflated costs.  

### **Technology (+$69,118 | +57.7%)**  
Technology expenses rose due to higher-than-anticipated licensing fees for cloud storage and analytics tools. The department upgraded its subscription tier for Workday Inc. ($424,344) to accommodate increased data processing needs for the PAP. Additionally, unbudgeted costs were incurred for integrating third-party APIs to enhance real-time data ingestion capabilities.  

### **Professional Services (+$20,989 | +42.7%)**  
Professional services costs were driven by consulting fees for external data architects engaged to expedite the PAP rollout. While this was not fully accounted for in the original budget, the accelerated timeline positions the department to deliver on strategic objectives ahead of schedule.  

### **Travel & Expense (+$9,422 | +37.8%)**  
Travel costs increased due to in-person workshops and vendor negotiations tied to the PAP initiative. These sessions were critical for knowledge transfer and ensuring alignment with external partners.
