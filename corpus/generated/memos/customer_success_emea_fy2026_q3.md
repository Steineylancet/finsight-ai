### **Executive Summary**  
The FY2026 Q3 forecast for the Customer Success - EMEA department projects total expenses of $1,163,888, exceeding the budget of $1,138,650 by $25,239 (+2.2%). This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-planned People Costs (+$23,956, +3.9%) and incremental increases in Facilities & Occupancy (+$3,269, +2.8%) and Professional Services (+$1,276, +2.2%). These increases are partially offset by modest savings in Technology (-$3,891, -2.2%). The forecast reflects strategic investments in talent and operational infrastructure to support customer retention and growth initiatives across the EMEA region.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **People Costs**: Forecasted at $644,944, exceeding the budget by $23,956 (+3.9%). This increase reflects planned hires to support expanded customer onboarding and account management efforts, as well as anticipated merit adjustments in line with market benchmarks.  
- **Technology**: Forecasted at $169,360, underspending the budget by $3,891 (-2.2%). Savings are attributed to renegotiated vendor contracts and deferred upgrades for non-critical tools.  
- **Travel & Expense**: Projected at $172,457, slightly above budget by $628 (+0.4%). This reflects increased travel activity to deepen customer engagement in priority markets, offset by disciplined cost controls.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $118,115, exceeding budget by $3,269 (+2.8%). The variance is driven by higher utility costs and minor facility upgrades to accommodate team expansion.  
- **Professional Services**: Projected at $59,012, over budget by $1,276 (+2.2%). This increase reflects additional consulting support for key customer success initiatives, including process optimization and training programs.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
1. **Headcount Expansion**: Hiring plans include three new Customer Success Managers to address rising customer demand and support retention efforts in high-growth markets.  
2. **Customer Engagement**: Increased travel reflects a strategic focus on in-person meetings to strengthen relationships with top-tier accounts.  
3. **Operational Efficiencies**: Technology savings result from proactive vendor negotiations and the postponement of non-critical system enhancements.  
4. **Facilities Investments**: Minor upgrades to office space are planned to support team productivity and accommodate new hires.  

---
