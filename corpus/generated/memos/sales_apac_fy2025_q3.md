### **Executive Summary**  
The APAC Sales department incurred total expenses of $30.84M in FY2025 Q3, exceeding the budget of $28.33M by $2.52M (+8.9%). Key drivers of the variance include higher-than-expected people costs, travel expenses, and technology investments. While several categories remain on track, the overall expense overrun reflects strategic investments in talent acquisition, training, and operational expansion to support growth in the region. Renewals and new business expenses, which are directly tied to revenue generation, performed within acceptable thresholds.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals of $2.71M exceeded the budget by $777.7K (+40.2%).  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $1.11M exceeded the budget by $360.6K (+47.9%).  
3. **Technology**: Actuals of $571.5K exceeded the budget by $191.9K (+50.5%).  
4. **Marketing**: Actuals of $551.7K exceeded the budget by $170.8K (+44.8%).  
5. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals of $544.9K exceeded the budget by $170.1K (+45.4%).  
6. **Renewals**: Actuals of $14.35M exceeded the budget by $552.2K (+4.0%).  
7. **New Business**: Actuals of $5.96M exceeded the budget by $189.5K (+3.3%).  
8. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $3.13M exceeded the budget by $144.5K (+4.8%).  
9. **Consulting**: Actuals of $1.91M came in below budget by $41.8K (-2.1%).  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+40.2%)**: The $777.7K overrun was driven by a 12% increase in headcount to support regional expansion efforts, particularly in high-growth markets like Southeast Asia. Additionally, investments in LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($1.22M), Workday Inc. ($1.12M), and Udemy Business ($997K) reflect a focus on talent acquisition and upskilling initiatives.  
   
2. **Travel & Expense (+47.9%)**: The $360.6K variance can be attributed to increased travel for client acquisition and retention efforts, particularly in emerging markets. Seasonal conferences and trade shows also contributed to elevated travel costs.  

3. **Technology (+50.5%)**: The $191.9K overrun reflects unplanned investments in sales enablement tools and CRM upgrades to enhance productivity and streamline operations.  

4. **Marketing (+44.8%)**: The $170.8K variance is linked to higher spending on digital campaigns and localized marketing efforts to capture market share in competitive regions.
