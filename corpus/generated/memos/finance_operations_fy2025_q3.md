### **Executive Summary**  
The Finance Operations department concluded FY2025 Q3 with total expenses of $2,362,789, exceeding the budget of $1,621,129 by $741,660 (+45.7%). This variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs, Professional Services, and Technology expenses. While some of these overages reflect strategic investments, others require closer scrutiny to ensure cost discipline in future quarters.  

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals of $1,308,621 exceeded the budget by $417,619 (+46.9%).  
2. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $466,223 exceeded the budget by $142,719 (+44.1%).  
3. **Technology**: Actuals of $284,164 exceeded the budget by $88,786 (+45.4%).  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals of $188,243 exceeded the budget by $58,494 (+45.1%).  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $115,537 exceeded the budget by $34,042 (+41.8%).  

All categories reported unfavorable variances, with People Costs contributing 56% of the total overage.  

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
- **People Costs (+$417,619 / +46.9%)**: The variance was driven by a 12% increase in headcount to support the implementation of the new Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system. Additionally, vendor costs for Principal Financial Group ($569,990) and ADP Payroll Services ($511,817) increased due to onboarding fees and higher payroll processing volumes. Investments in employee training through Udemy Business ($374,279) also contributed to the overage, reflecting our commitment to upskilling staff for the ERP rollout.  

- **Professional Services (+$142,719 / +44.1%)**: This variance was driven by consulting fees related to the ERP implementation and compliance support for new regulatory requirements. While these costs were higher than anticipated, they are expected to taper off in FY2025 Q4 as key project milestones are completed.  

- **Technology (+$88,786 / +45.4%)**: Higher-than-budgeted expenditures stemmed from software licensing for the ERP system and incremental costs for cybersecurity enhancements. While these investments were necessary to modernize our infrastructure, they exceeded initial estimates due to vendor price escalation.  

- **Facilities & Occupancy (+$58,494 / +45.1%)**: The variance reflects temporary office space rentals to accommodate project teams working on the ERP implementation. These costs are expected to normalize in FY2025 Q4 as teams transition back to our main facilities.  

- **Travel & Expense (+$34,042 / +41.8%)**: Increased travel was driven by site visits for ERP training sessions and vendor negotiations. While these activities were essential, the associated costs exceeded budget expectations.
