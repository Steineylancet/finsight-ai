### **Executive Summary**  
The Customer Success EMEA team is forecasted to close FY2026 Q4 with total expenses of $1,424,596, reflecting a modest 0.9% ($13,034) increase over the budget of $1,411,562. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated investments in Technology and Facilities & Occupancy, partially offset by minor savings in Travel & Expense. These adjustments align with strategic initiatives to enhance operational efficiency and support customer retention efforts in the region.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $781,727, this category remains effectively on budget (+0.1%, $576). The slight increase is attributed to planned merit adjustments and onboarding costs for two new hires in Q4 to support the growing customer base.  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $209,401, this category reflects a 0.4% ($822) reduction versus budget. The decrease is tied to a shift toward virtual engagements for Q4 customer workshops, reducing travel requirements.  
3. **Technology**: Forecasted at $215,734, exceeding budget by 2.7% ($5,755). This increase is driven by the renewal of a key customer relationship management (CRM) platform and incremental costs for enhanced analytics tools to support proactive customer success strategies.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $146,097, up 4.0% ($5,607) versus budget. The variance is linked to higher utility costs and lease escalations for the EMEA regional hub.  
5. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $71,636, representing a 2.8% ($1,919) increase over budget. This reflects additional consulting support for a Q4 initiative to optimize customer onboarding processes.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Headcount Growth**: Two planned hires in Q4 will expand the team’s capacity to manage increased customer demand.  
- **Technology Investments**: The CRM renewal and analytics upgrades are expected to drive long-term efficiency and customer retention.  
- **Seasonality**: Q4 typically sees higher engagement levels with enterprise customers, necessitating additional professional services support.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Technology Costs**: Any delays in vendor negotiations for the CRM renewal could result in higher-than-forecasted expenses.  
2. **Facilities Costs**: Unanticipated maintenance or utility rate increases could further inflate Facilities & Occupancy expenses.  
3. **Travel Volatility**: While savings are currently forecasted, any last-minute in-person customer escalations could increase Travel & Expense costs.  

---

The Customer Success EMEA team’s forecast reflects a disciplined approach to resource allocation while supporting strategic priorities. We will continue to monitor risks and provide updates as necessary.
