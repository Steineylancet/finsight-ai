### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q3, the Sales - APAC team is forecasted to exceed its budget by $929,140 (+2.9%), with total forecasted spend projected at $33,281,615. This increase is driven by stronger-than-anticipated performance in Renewals (+3.3%) and New Business (+2.9%), as well as higher Professional Services costs (+5.5%) tied to ongoing customer projects. While most categories are tracking close to budget, People Costs are forecasted to come in slightly under budget (-2.8%), reflecting slower-than-expected hiring. This memo outlines the planned spend by category, key assumptions underpinning the forecast, and potential risks that may impact Q3 performance.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **Renewals**: Forecasted at $16,333,807, up $516,395 (+3.3%) from budget, reflecting strong customer retention and upsell opportunities in key accounts.  
- **New Business**: Expected to reach $6,886,632, exceeding budget by $191,643 (+2.9%) due to robust pipeline activity and anticipated deal closures in high-growth markets like Southeast Asia.  
- **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $3,652,432, up $191,478 (+5.5%), driven by increased demand for implementation and training services tied to new client wins.  
- **Consulting**: Projected at $2,316,703, a modest increase of $55,347 (+2.4%), reflecting additional advisory support for strategic initiatives.  
- **People Costs**: Expected to come in at $2,007,308, undershooting budget by $58,237 (-2.8%) due to delayed hiring for planned roles in sales and account management.  
- **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $828,815, a minor increase of $7,621 (+0.9%), reflecting seasonal upticks in travel for client meetings and regional events.  
- **Marketing**: On track with budget at $416,617, with no material variances expected.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy**: Projected at $424,269, up $16,194 (+4.0%), driven by incremental costs for temporary office expansions in growth regions.  
- **Technology**: Forecasted at $415,030, up $8,686 (+2.1%) due to expanded software licenses for CRM and analytics tools.  

---
