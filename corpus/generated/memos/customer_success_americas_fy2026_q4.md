## **Executive Summary**  
The FY2026 Q4 forecast for the Customer Success – Americas department projects total expenses of $2,800,573, exceeding the budget of $2,698,768 by $101,805 (+3.8%). This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs and Travel & Expense, partially offset by savings in Technology. The forecast reflects strategic investments to support customer retention and expansion initiatives, as well as adjustments for business-critical activities in the quarter.  

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $1,556,283, exceeding the budget by $79,023 (+5.3%). This increase accounts for planned headcount additions to support customer onboarding and retention efforts, as well as higher-than-expected incentive payouts tied to strong Q4 performance metrics.  
2. **Technology**: Forecasted at $400,479, coming in $11,710 (-2.8%) under budget. Savings are attributed to deferred renewals for non-critical software tools and renegotiated vendor contracts.  
3. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $429,108, exceeding budget by $26,623 (+6.6%). This reflects increased travel activity for in-person customer engagements and end-of-year account reviews, which are critical for retention and upsell opportunities.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $277,753, slightly over budget by $5,850 (+2.2%), driven by incremental costs for temporary workspace expansions to accommodate new hires.  
5. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $136,950, marginally over budget by $2,018 (+1.5%), due to additional consulting support for process optimization initiatives.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Headcount Growth**: The forecast assumes onboarding of 5 additional Customer Success Managers in Q4 to address increased customer demand and improve coverage ratios.  
- **Seasonality & Customer Engagement**: Q4 is historically a peak period for customer renewals and upsell opportunities, necessitating higher travel and engagement-related expenses.  
- **Technology Optimization**: Savings in Technology reflect a strategic decision to delay non-essential software upgrades until FY2027.  
- **Workspace Adjustments**: Temporary workspace expansions are planned to accommodate new hires in key regional hubs.  

---

1. **People Costs Sensitivity**: The forecast assumes timely onboarding of planned hires. Delays could impact customer engagement metrics and retention targets.
