### **Executive Summary**  
The Americas Sales division closed FY2025 Q4 with total expenses of $55.1M, under budget by $3.3M (-5.6%). This favorable variance was primarily driven by strategic cost containment across discretionary categories, including Technology, Facilities, Marketing, and Travel. However, higher-than-expected spending in New Business (+5.2%) and Renewals (+3.1%) partially offset these savings. These variances reflect increased client acquisition efforts and a strong renewal cycle, aligning with our strategic growth priorities.  

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
Key expense categories performed as follows:  
- **Renewals**: Actuals of $29.9M exceeded the $29.0M budget by $0.9M (+3.1%), driven by higher renewal volumes and associated vendor costs.  
- **New Business**: Actuals of $14.5M were $0.7M (+5.2%) over budget, reflecting elevated investment in client acquisition initiatives.  
- **Professional Services**: Actuals of $6.4M were $0.2M (+2.4%) above budget, primarily due to extended project timelines with key vendors.  
- **Consulting**: Actuals of $4.3M were on track with budget (+0.1%).  
- **Discretionary Categories (Technology, Facilities, Marketing, Travel, People Costs)**: Combined savings of $5.1M (-100%) reflect the strategic decision to defer non-essential expenditures in Q4.  

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **Renewals (+$0.9M)**: The variance was driven by higher-than-anticipated renewal activity, particularly with large accounts like Meridian Financial Group (+$97K over budget). This reflects strong client retention but also increased commission payouts and vendor support costs.  
2. **New Business (+$0.7M)**: The division invested heavily in Q4 to capitalize on year-end growth opportunities, including expanded lead generation campaigns and additional sales enablement resources. While this exceeded budget, it aligns with our strategic focus on expanding market share.  
3. **Professional Services (+$0.2M)**: This variance stems from extended engagements with Vantage Capital Holdings (+$60K) and other vendors to support sales operations during a high-demand quarter.  
4. **Discretionary Categories (-$5.1M)**: Savings in Technology, Facilities, Marketing, Travel, and People Costs reflect a deliberate pause on non-critical projects and hiring. This decision was made to mitigate potential overages in variable categories like New Business and Renewals.  

### **Key Vendors**  
Top vendor relationships in Q4 include:  
- **Vantage Capital Holdings**: $72K across Consulting and Professional Services, driven by extended project scopes.  
- **Meridian Financial Group**: $97K over budget in Renewals, reflecting increased support for key account retention.  
- **Ironside Technologies Corp**: $62K in Consulting, supporting CRM optimization efforts.
