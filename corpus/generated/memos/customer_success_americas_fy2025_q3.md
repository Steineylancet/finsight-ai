### **Executive Summary**  
The Customer Success - Americas department reported total expenses of $2,905,958 in FY2025 Q3, exceeding the budget of $2,042,792 by $863,166 (+42.3%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$452,214), Technology investments (+$148,190), and Travel & Expense (+$133,116). While these variances reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition, technology upgrades, and client engagement, they underscore the need for tighter cost controls in upcoming quarters.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs:** Actuals of $1,574,074 exceeded the budget of $1,121,860 by $452,214 (+40.3%).  
2. **Technology:** Actuals of $452,762 were $148,190 (+48.7%) over the budget of $304,572.  
3. **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $442,908 surpassed the budget of $309,792 by $133,116 (+43.0%).  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy:** Actuals of $288,944 exceeded the budget of $204,316 by $84,628 (+41.4%).  
5. **Professional Services:** Actuals of $147,271 were $45,018 (+44.0%) over the budget of $102,252.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+$452,214 / +40.3%)**  
   The variance in People Costs reflects a deliberate investment in headcount to support a 15% YoY increase in customer accounts. Significant costs were incurred through LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($517,488) and Principal Financial Group ($611,555) for recruitment and onboarding of 25 new Customer Success Managers (CSMs). Additionally, healthcare premiums with Aetna ($468,637) rose due to higher employee participation in family health plans.  

2. **Technology (+$148,190 / +48.7%)**  
   The department implemented a significant upgrade to its customer relationship management (CRM) platform, leveraging Cornerstone OnDemand ($386,934) for enhanced analytics and reporting capabilities. This investment aligns with the broader strategy to improve customer retention and satisfaction metrics but was not fully anticipated in the original budget.  

3. **Travel & Expense (+$133,116 / +43.0%)**  
   Increased travel expenses reflect heightened client engagement activities, including on-site visits for Q3 renewals and upselling opportunities. Additionally, Q3 saw the launch of a customer roadshow initiative targeting key accounts in the U.S. and Canada.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$84,628 / +41.4%)**  
   The variance in Facilities & Occupancy stems from temporary office expansions in Dallas and Toronto to accommodate new hires and support regional client events.  

5. **Professional Services (+$45,018 / +44.0%)**  
   Higher-than-expected spending on consulting services for process automation and workflow optimization contributed to this variance.
