### **Executive Summary**  
The FY2026 Q2 forecast for the Sales APAC department projects total expenditures of $39.1M, representing a $991K (+2.6%) increase over the $38.1M budget. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated investments in New Business, People Costs, and Consulting, which align with strategic growth initiatives in the region. The forecast reflects a balanced approach to supporting revenue growth while managing operational efficiency.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
Key expense categories driving the forecast include:  
1. **Renewals**: Projected at $18.6M, slightly exceeding budget by $207K (+1.1%) due to accelerated renewal cycles for key enterprise accounts.  
2. **New Business**: Forecasted at $9.2M, $414K (+4.7%) above budget, reflecting increased sales activity and pipeline expansion efforts.  
3. **Professional Services**: Estimated at $4.0M, $66K (+1.7%) over budget, driven by ongoing client onboarding and implementation projects.  
4. **People Costs**: Expected to reach $2.3M, $122K (+5.6%) above budget, as planned headcount additions in sales and account management ramp up.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Projected at $908K, $45K (+5.2%) above budget, reflecting increased travel to support in-person client engagements and regional events.  

Other categories, including Marketing, Technology, and Facilities, show moderate increases, collectively contributing to the overall variance.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
1. **Strategic Growth Initiatives**: The forecast assumes continued investment in customer acquisition and retention, with a focus on high-growth markets such as Southeast Asia and Australia.  
2. **Headcount Expansion**: Planned hiring of additional sales representatives and account managers is expected to bolster revenue generation and deepen client relationships.  
3. **Seasonality**: Q2 typically experiences heightened activity in renewals and new business, aligning with fiscal year-end procurement cycles for many clients.  
4. **Client Engagements**: Increased travel and consulting spend reflect a strategic emphasis on strengthening client partnerships and delivering tailored solutions.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Macroeconomic Uncertainty**: Potential economic headwinds in the APAC region could impact client budgets and delay purchasing decisions.  
2. **Hiring Timelines**: Delays in onboarding planned hires may constrain revenue growth and increase workload for existing staff.  
3. **Cost Management**: While investments are aligned with growth objectives, close monitoring of discretionary spend (e.g., Travel & Expense, Consulting) will be critical to maintain budget discipline.  
4. **Renewal Rates**: Any deviations from forecasted renewal performance could materially impact revenue projections and associated expenses.
