### **Executive Summary**  
The Sales - Americas department closed FY2025 Q2 with total expenses of $56.97M, exceeding the budget of $53.64M by $3.33M (+6.2%). While revenue-driving categories such as Renewals (+2.2%) and New Business (+3.7%) remained on track, significant cost overruns occurred in People Costs (+47.6%), Travel & Expense (+45.2%), and Facilities & Occupancy (+48.3%). These variances were primarily driven by headcount expansion, increased client engagement activities, and temporary office space expansions to support regional growth initiatives.  

Despite these cost pressures, operational investments are aligned with strategic objectives, including talent acquisition, market penetration, and customer retention. However, cost containment measures will be necessary in H2 to mitigate risks of further budget deviations.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals reached $3.23M, exceeding the $2.19M budget by $1.04M (+47.6%).

3. **New Business**: Actuals were $13.17M, exceeding the $12.69M budget by $475K (+3.7%).  
4. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals totaled $1.28M, surpassing the $884K budget by $400K (+45.2%).  
5. **Professional Services**: Actuals were $5.81M, exceeding the $5.55M budget by $258K (+4.7%).  
6. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals reached $652K, above the $440K budget by $212K (+48.3%).  
7. **Technology**: Actuals were $638K, exceeding the $444K budget by $194K (+43.7%).  
8. **Marketing**: Actuals totaled $635K, above the $442K budget by $193K (+43.6%).  
9. **Consulting**: Actuals were $3.95M, under budget by $30K (-0.7%).
