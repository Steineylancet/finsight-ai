### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (-$1,450,633, -100.0%)**  
   The most significant variance stems from People Costs, which were budgeted at $1.45M. This is primarily due to a temporary hiring freeze implemented in Q3 as part of the company’s broader cost optimization strategy. Additionally, several planned executive hires were deferred to FY2026 Q1 to align with the anticipated launch of new strategic initiatives.  

2. **Travel & Expense (-$418,108, -100.0%)**  
   Travel & Expense savings were driven by the postponement of key leadership conferences and offsite strategy sessions, originally scheduled for Q4, to early FY2026. This decision reflects both cost prudence and logistical considerations tied to vendor availability and scheduling conflicts.  

3. **Professional Services (-$244,139, -100.0%)**  
   Professional Services expenses were budgeted for consulting engagements to support strategic planning and M&A due diligence. These engagements were delayed as the company prioritized internal resources and shifted timelines for key projects.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (-$164,740, -100.0%)**  
   Facilities-related expenses were minimized due to the continued use of hybrid work arrangements. Additionally, a planned office space expansion was deferred pending further evaluation of long-term space requirements
