## **Executive Summary**  
The Marketing – Americas department reported total expenses of $3,630,340 in FY2025 Q1, exceeding the budget of $2,497,077 by $1,133,263 (+45.4%). This variance is primarily driven by increased investments in people costs, professional services, and marketing technology. While these expenditures reflect strategic initiatives to drive growth in key markets, they also highlight the need for tighter cost controls and improved forecasting.  

Key drivers of the overage include expanded headcount to support new campaigns, vendor spend tied to enhanced digital marketing capabilities, and higher-than-expected costs for professional services. As we move into Q2, mitigating risks tied to vendor management and aligning spend with expected ROI will be critical.

---

## **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **Marketing**: Actuals of $1,445,985 exceeded the $997,281 budget by $448,704 (+45.0%). Elevated costs stemmed from increased vendor spend on digital advertising platforms and event management software.  
2. **People Costs**: Actuals of $1,117,898 surpassed the $752,348 budget by $365,551 (+48.6%). This reflects the onboarding of additional marketing staff to support regional campaigns and a one-time severance payout from restructuring efforts.  
3. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $529,374 exceeded the $374,290 budget by $155,084 (+41.4%), driven by expanded reliance on external agencies for public relations and content development.  
4. **Technology**: Actuals of $365,456 exceeded the $248,209 budget by $117,247 (+47.2%). This variance reflects upfront costs for new marketing automation tools, including Marketo and HubSpot.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $171,627 exceeded the $124,948 budget by $46,678 (+37.4%), due to higher-than-expected travel for in-person client events and conferences.  

---

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+$365,551)**: The variance is largely attributable to a 10% headcount increase in Q1 to support new product launches in the U.S. and Canada. Additionally, severance costs from a restructuring initiative accounted for $90,000 of the overage.  
2. **Marketing (+$448,704)**: Elevated vendor spend with Cvent Inc ($458,220), Marketo ($457,502), and LinkedIn Marketing ($380,870) drove this variance. These investments supported a major regional campaign targeting high-growth industries, though ROI metrics are still being evaluated.  
3. **Technology (+$117,247)**: The variance reflects upfront licensing costs for Marketo and HubSpot, which were not fully accounted for in the Q1 budget. These tools are expected to improve lead generation and campaign tracking capabilities.  
4. **Professional Services (+$155,084)**: Edelman PR ($452,159) was engaged for a high-profile media campaign, which incurred higher-than-anticipated costs.
