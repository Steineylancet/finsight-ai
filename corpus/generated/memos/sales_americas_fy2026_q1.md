### **Executive Summary**  
The Sales - Americas department reported total expenses of $49.63M for FY2026 Q1, exceeding the budget of $44.88M by $4.75M (+10.6%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected costs in Renewals (+$2.01M, +8.9%), People Costs (+$850K, +41.8%), and New Business (+$606K, +6.3%). While these variances reflect increased investment in growth initiatives and workforce expansion, they also highlight areas requiring tighter cost controls.  

Despite the overages, the department achieved significant progress in expanding its customer base and retaining key accounts, which are expected to drive revenue growth in subsequent quarters.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  

1. **Renewals:** Actuals of $24.65M exceeded the budget by $2.01M (+8.9%).  
2. **People Costs:** Actuals of $2.88M were $850K (+41.8%) above budget.  
3. **New Business:** Actuals of $10.16M were $606K (+6.3%) above budget.  
4. **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $1.16M exceeded the budget by $343K (+41.8%).  
5. **Consulting:** Actuals of $3.57M were $211K (+6.3%) over budget.  
6. **Professional Services:** Actuals of $5.44M were $188K (+3.6%) over budget but remained on track.  
7. **Technology, Marketing, and Facilities:** These categories collectively exceeded budget by $542K, with variances ranging from +43% to +45%.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  

1. **Renewals (+$2.01M, +8.9%):** Higher-than-anticipated contract renewal costs stemmed from increased incentive payouts to retain top-tier clients. This aligns with a strategic push to secure multi-year agreements in a competitive market.  

2. **People Costs (+$850K, +41.8%):** The variance reflects headcount growth to support expanded sales territories, as well as increased benefits costs. Notably, top vendors such as Aetna Health Insurance ($1.35M) and Indeed.com ($1.22M) contributed to this overage due to higher recruitment and healthcare expenses.  

3. **New Business (+$606K, +6.3%):** Investments in lead generation and sales enablement tools drove this overage. While above budget, these costs are tied to initiatives that have already yielded a 12% increase in pipeline opportunities.  

4. **Travel & Expense (+$343K, +41.8%):** The resumption of in-person client meetings and industry events contributed to higher travel costs. This increase was anticipated as part of a post-pandemic return to normal business operations.
