### **Executive Summary**  
The EMEA Sales department reported total expenses of $30.85M in FY2025 Q3, exceeding the budget of $28.64M by $2.21M (+7.7%). While revenue growth initiatives have driven some of this variance, the overages highlight areas requiring tighter cost controls. Key drivers include higher-than-anticipated People Costs (+44.8%), Travel & Expense (+47.7%), and Marketing (+50.0%). Renewals and Consulting expenses remained on track, reflecting disciplined execution in these areas. Immediate focus is needed to address cost escalations in discretionary categories without compromising growth momentum.

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
- **People Costs (+$622.8K, +44.8%)**: The variance is attributed to a 15% headcount increase to support aggressive new business growth targets and backfilling key vacancies. Additionally, higher vendor costs from Indeed.com ($632K) and Workday Inc. ($612K) reflect expanded recruitment efforts and HR system upgrades.  
- **Travel & Expense (+$263.6K, +47.7%)**: Increased travel was driven by intensified client engagement in high-growth markets such as Germany and the UAE. This aligns with our strategic push to secure large enterprise accounts but underscores the need for stricter travel policy enforcement.
