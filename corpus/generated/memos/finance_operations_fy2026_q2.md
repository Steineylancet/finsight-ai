## Executive Summary  
The Finance Operations department is projected to close FY2026 Q2 with total expenses of $1,904,725, reflecting a forecasted overspend of $25,596 (+1.4%) against the approved budget of $1,879,130. While People Costs are expected to remain tightly aligned with budget (-0.1%), upward variances in Professional Services (+4.1%) and Technology (+3.5%) are the primary drivers of the forecasted variance. These increases are tied to strategic initiatives and critical investments in operational efficiency.  

This memo outlines the planned spend by category, key assumptions underpinning the forecast, and potential risks to monitor as we approach the quarter.  

---

## Planned Spend by Category  
1. **People Costs** ($1,035,708; -0.1% vs. budget):  
   Forecasted People Costs reflect stable headcount, with no significant changes in hiring plans for Q2. Minor savings are anticipated due to timing adjustments in backfilling open roles.  

2. **Professional Services** ($390,984; +4.1% vs. budget):  
   The forecasted overspend is driven by increased reliance on external consultants to support a financial systems upgrade project and compliance-related deliverables. This is a planned, time-bound investment to ensure project milestones are met.  

3. **Technology** ($232,439; +3.5% vs. budget):  
   Higher-than-budgeted spend is driven by the renewal of critical software licenses and incremental costs associated with scaling cloud-based tools to support growing transaction volumes.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy** ($150,856; +1.7% vs. budget):  
   The forecast reflects modest increases due to inflationary adjustments in lease agreements and higher utility costs during the summer months.  

5. **Travel & Expense** ($94,739; +1.3% vs. budget):  
   The variance is attributed to increased travel for leadership team offsites and finance training programs scheduled for Q2.  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  
- **Strategic Projects:** The financial systems upgrade is a key driver of higher Professional Services and Technology spend. This project is expected to enhance reporting capabilities and automate manual workflows.  
- **Seasonality:** Travel and utility costs are seasonally higher in Q2, as anticipated in prior planning cycles.  
- **Inflationary Pressures:** Modest increases in Facilities & Occupancy costs reflect broader market trends.  

---

## Risks & Watch Items  
- **Project Timelines:** Delays in the financial systems upgrade could lead to additional consulting costs. Close monitoring of project milestones is critical.  
- **Vendor Pricing:** Renewals for key software licenses are subject to final negotiations, which could result in higher-than-forecasted Technology costs.  
- **Travel Policy Compliance:** Increased travel activity necessitates strict adherence to expense policies to mitigate potential overspend.
