### **Executive Summary**  
In FY2026 Q1, the Finance Department’s total expenses reached $1,007,230, exceeding the budget of $725,101 by $282,129 (+38.9%). This variance was driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs, Professional Services, and Technology expenditures, reflecting strategic investments in talent acquisition, system upgrades, and external consulting to support key initiatives. While these investments position us for long-term growth, they require close monitoring in subsequent quarters to align with annual targets.

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals totaled $546,896, exceeding the budget of $402,649 by $144,247 (+35.8%).  
2. **Professional Services**: Actuals reached $199,043, surpassing the budget of $142,288 by $56,755 (+39.9%).  
3. **Technology**: Actuals amounted to $124,484, exceeding the budget of $87,290 by $37,193 (+42.6%).  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals were $85,893, above the budget of $57,107 by $28,786 (+50.4%).  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals totaled $50,915, exceeding the budget of $35,768 by $15,147 (+42.3%).  

All categories reported unfavorable variances, with the most significant impacts in People Costs and Professional Services.

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
- **People Costs (+$144,247 / +35.8%)**: The variance reflects higher-than-expected headcount growth and associated benefits costs. Notably, $297,853 was paid to MetLife Insurance and $129,342 to Workday Inc. for benefits administration and payroll system enhancements. Additionally, $95,886 was allocated to Indeed.com for an aggressive Q1 hiring campaign to support new strategic initiatives in risk management and financial modeling.  
- **Professional Services (+$56,755 / +39.9%)**: The increase stems from engaging external consultants to support compliance with new regulatory requirements and to streamline reporting processes. These services were critical to meeting tight deadlines for FY2025 year-end audits and preparing for upcoming ESG disclosure mandates.  
- **Technology (+$37,193 / +42.6%)**: Investments in cloud-based financial systems and cybersecurity upgrades drove this variance. These initiatives are part of a broader effort to modernize our infrastructure and mitigate data security risks.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy (+$28,786 / +50.4%)**: The variance reflects temporary office expansions to accommodate new hires and increased utility costs during the winter months.  
- **Travel & Expense (+$15,147 / +42.3%)**: Higher travel costs were primarily driven by increased client-facing activities and attendance at industry conferences to strengthen relationships and explore growth opportunities.
