### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q1, the Executive Department reported total expenses of $2,745,840, exceeding the budget of $1,936,360 by $809,481 (+41.8%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$473,295, +40.9%) and Travel & Expense (+$158,254, +45.1%). These variances reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition, leadership development, and increased travel related to key business initiatives. While these costs are above plan, they align with our long-term growth objectives and ongoing transformation efforts.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals totaled $1,631,582, exceeding the budget of $1,158,287 by $473,295 (+40.9%).  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals were $509,276, over budget by $158,254 (+45.1%).  
3. **Professional Services**: Actuals reached $284,385, surpassing the budget by $87,748 (+44.6%).  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals were $185,440, exceeding the budget by $50,759 (+37.7%).  
5. **Insurance & Risk**: Actuals totaled $135,158, over budget by $39,425 (+41.2%).  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+$473,295, +40.9%)**  
   The primary driver of this variance was higher-than-anticipated spending on talent acquisition and retention initiatives. Notably, vendor payments to MetLife Insurance ($468,605), Workday Inc. ($443,673), and Indeed.com ($320,199) reflect investments in employee benefits, HR systems, and recruitment efforts. Additionally, the onboarding of senior executives to support strategic growth initiatives contributed to increased payroll costs through ADP Payroll Services ($317,401).  

2. **Travel & Expense (+$158,254, +45.1%)**  
   Increased travel expenses were driven by executive participation in industry conferences and client engagement activities, particularly in expanding markets. Several unplanned international trips related to a new partnership initiative also contributed to this variance.  

3. **Professional Services (+$87,748, +44.6%)**  
   The variance in this category reflects higher-than-expected spending on external consulting services to support organizational restructuring and strategic planning. These engagements were critical to positioning Crestwood Capital Group for future growth.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$50,759, +37.7%)**  
   This variance was due to temporary office expansions to accommodate new hires and increased usage of co-working spaces for offsite executive meetings.  

5. **Insurance & Risk (+$39,425, +41.2%)**  
   Higher insurance premiums, particularly from Cigna Healthcare ($303,665), were driven by expanded coverage for new hires and adjustments to group benefit plans.
