## Executive Summary  
For FY2025 Q3, IT Infrastructure expenses totaled $5,006,257, exceeding the budget of $3,343,114 by $1,663,143 (+49.7%). This unfavorable variance was driven by increased spending across all major categories, particularly Technology (+$812,781, +54.6%) and People Costs (+$577,222, +48.9%). These variances reflect strategic investments in critical infrastructure upgrades, expanded headcount to support new initiatives, and higher-than-anticipated reliance on professional services. While these expenditures position us for long-term operational resilience and scalability, they highlight the need for tighter cost controls in future quarters.  

---

## Expense Performance by Category  
1. **Technology**: Actuals of $2,300,327 exceeded the budget by $812,781 (+54.6%). This was primarily driven by increased licensing costs for key platforms such as Salesforce ($419,941) and ServiceNow ($416,465), as well as expanded cybersecurity measures through CrowdStrike Holdings ($431,922).  
2. **People Costs**: Actuals of $1,757,425 were $577,222 (+48.9%) over budget. This reflects headcount growth to support our cloud migration project and expanded training programs delivered via Cornerstone OnDemand ($407,056).  
3. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $565,433 exceeded the budget by $159,243 (+39.2%). This was due to higher-than-expected consulting fees for the data center optimization project and external support for compliance audits.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals of $241,048 were $73,829 (+44.2%) over budget, driven by temporary co-location space rentals to support the transition of on-premise servers to the cloud.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $142,023 were $40,068 (+39.3%) over budget, reflecting increased travel for vendor negotiations and on-site project management for regional data center upgrades.  

---

## Key Variances & Commentary  
The most significant variance occurred in **Technology**, where unplanned costs arose from expedited software licensing renewals and scaling cloud infrastructure to accommodate higher data volumes. For example, Amazon Web Services (AWS) expenses reached $395,295 as we expanded storage and computing capacity to support new analytics tools.  

**People Costs** were impacted by the onboarding of 12 new FTEs to support the cloud migration and cybersecurity initiatives. Additionally, the rollout of a company-wide IT training program increased costs for Cornerstone OnDemand’s learning management platform.  

In **Professional Services**, we engaged external consultants to accelerate the data center optimization project, which incurred higher-than-budgeted fees but allowed us to meet critical milestones ahead of schedule.
