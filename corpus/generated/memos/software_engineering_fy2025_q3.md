### **Executive Summary**  
The Software Engineering department reported total expenses of $10.95M in FY2025 Q3, exceeding the budget of $7.43M by $3.52M (+47.3%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$2.34M, +48.5%) and Technology expenses (+$681K, +46.0%). These increases reflect strategic investments in workforce expansion, employee benefits, and critical technology upgrades to support the company’s accelerated digital transformation initiatives. While the overspend warrants close monitoring, these expenditures align with the department's long-term capacity-building goals.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals reached $7.18M, exceeding the $4.84M budget by $2.34M (+48.5%). This variance reflects a 15% increase in headcount to support the launch of two new product development teams, coupled with higher-than-anticipated employee benefits costs driven by vendor rate adjustments.  
2. **Technology**: Spending totaled $2.16M, surpassing the $1.48M budget by $681K (+46.0%). Key drivers include the deployment of new cloud infrastructure and software licenses to enhance scalability and security.  
3. **Professional Services**: Expenses of $854K exceeded the $593K budget by $262K (+44.1%), largely due to increased reliance on external consultants for specialized AI and machine learning projects.  
4. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $533K were $163K (+44.0%) over budget, reflecting increased travel for team collaboration and industry conferences as in-person events resumed post-pandemic.  
5. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Costs of $218K exceeded the $150K budget by $68K (+44.9%), driven by temporary office expansions to accommodate new hires.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
- **People Costs**: The $2.34M variance was primarily influenced by higher vendor costs from top providers, including Workday Inc. ($2.71M) and MetLife Insurance ($2.41M). These increases stemmed from renegotiated contracts to enhance employee benefits packages, including expanded health coverage through Cigna Healthcare ($2.27M).  
- **Technology**: The $681K overspend reflects strategic investments in cloud solutions and software tools, including expanded usage of Cornerstone OnDemand ($2.02M) for talent management and training initiatives.  
- **Professional Services**: The $262K variance is tied to the onboarding of external experts to accelerate development on high-priority AI features, which are expected to drive competitive differentiation.  

---

### **Key Vendors**  
The department’s top five vendors accounted for $11.24M in expenditures, with the majority allocated to People Costs. Notable vendors include:  
1. **Workday Inc.** ($2.71M): HR and payroll systems.  
2. **MetLife Insurance** ($2.41M): Employee life insurance.  
3. **Cigna Healthcare** ($2.27M): Health benefits.
