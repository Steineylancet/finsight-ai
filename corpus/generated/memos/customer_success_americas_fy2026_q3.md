## Executive Summary  
The Customer Success – Americas department is projected to end FY2026 Q3 with a total spend of $2,229,524, which is $3,765 (0.2%) under the approved budget of $2,233,289. While the overall forecast aligns closely with the budget, minor variances are anticipated across key categories. Notably, People Costs are projected slightly above budget due to planned headcount adjustments, while Technology and Travel & Expense categories are forecasted to come in below budget. These variances reflect deliberate cost management and evolving operational priorities.  

---

## Planned Spend by Category  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $1,232,505, representing a $3,166 (0.3%) increase over budget. This reflects planned hiring of two Customer Success Managers in early Q3 to support new client onboarding initiatives.  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $331,550, $4,427 (1.3%) below budget. Reduced travel spend is expected due to increased reliance on virtual client meetings and fewer in-person events during the quarter.  
3. **Technology**: Forecasted at $327,403, $5,964 (1.8%) under budget. This variance is driven by cost savings from renegotiated software contracts and deferred implementation of a new analytics tool to Q4.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $222,598, $540 (0.2%) under budget. No significant changes are expected in office-related expenses.  
5. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $115,468, $3,999 (3.6%) above budget. This increase reflects higher consulting costs tied to a Q3 customer retention project aimed at improving Net Promoter Scores (NPS).  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  
- **Headcount Growth**: The addition of two new hires in Q3 is expected to bolster client support capacity, driving a modest increase in People Costs.  
- **Cost Optimization**: Strategic efforts to renegotiate vendor contracts and defer non-critical technology upgrades are yielding savings in the Technology category.  
- **Travel Patterns**: A continued shift toward virtual engagement is expected to reduce Travel & Expense spending.  
- **Project-Based Spend**: Professional Services costs will increase due to a targeted initiative to enhance customer satisfaction and retention metrics.  

---

## Risks & Watch Items  
1. **Hiring Timeline**: Delays in onboarding the planned hires could impact client support capacity and overall People Costs.  
2. **Customer Retention Initiative**: The success of the Professional Services investment hinges on achieving measurable improvements in NPS and retention rates.  
3. **Technology Implementation**: Deferring the analytics tool implementation to Q4 may delay associated efficiency gains.
