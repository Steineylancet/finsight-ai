### **Executive Summary**  
In FY2025 Q3, the Marketing – Americas department reported total expenses of $4,052,751, exceeding the budget of $2,748,744 by $1,304,007 (+47.4%). This variance was driven by strategic investments in demand generation campaigns, unanticipated headcount growth, and increased reliance on professional services and technology to support new initiatives. While these investments have strengthened our competitive position, they have also highlighted areas requiring tighter cost controls and vendor management.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **Marketing Expenses**: Actuals reached $1,608,190, exceeding the $1,099,962 budget by $508,228 (+46.2%).  
2. **People Costs**: Actuals totaled $1,220,574, surpassing the $816,561 budget by $404,013 (+49.5%).  
3. **Professional Services**: Actuals were $617,753, over the $416,022 budget by $201,731 (+48.5%).  
4. **Technology**: Actuals came in at $405,947, exceeding the $278,786 budget by $127,161 (+45.6%).  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals totaled $200,287, above the $137,414 budget by $62,874 (+45.8%).  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
**1. Marketing Expenses (+46.2%, +$508,228):**  
The variance was primarily driven by expanded digital advertising campaigns on LinkedIn and HubSpot, which accounted for $576,363 and $657,480, respectively. These platforms were leveraged to support the launch of two new financial products targeting the mid-market segment. While the campaigns delivered strong engagement metrics, the upfront spend exceeded initial projections due to higher-than-expected CPM rates and additional A/B testing.  

**2. People Costs (+49.5%, +$404,013):**  
The variance reflects a 15% increase in headcount during Q3 to support the scaling of regional marketing operations. This included the onboarding of five senior-level hires specializing in digital strategy and analytics. Additionally, retention bonuses for key personnel and market-driven salary adjustments further contributed to the overage.  

**3. Professional Services (+48.5%, +$201,731):**  
Higher-than-budgeted spend on WPP Group ($492,716) and Cvent Inc ($481,882) drove this variance. WPP was engaged for a major rebranding initiative targeting institutional clients, while Cvent supported the execution of a high-profile client summit in Miami. Both projects incurred additional scope-related costs.  

**4. Technology (+45.6%, +$127,161):**  
Investments in marketing automation tools, specifically Marketo ($553,518), exceeded budget as the team expanded its use of advanced analytics and personalization features. These enhancements were critical to improving lead conversion rates but involved higher licensing fees and implementation costs.  

**5. Travel & Expense (+45.8%, +$62,874):**  
The variance reflects increased travel for client-facing events and internal strategy
