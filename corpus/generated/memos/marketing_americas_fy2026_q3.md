## **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q3, the Marketing Americas team is forecasted to spend $3,041,461, which represents a 2.6% increase (+$77,319) over the approved budget of $2,964,143. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated investments in technology, professional services, and people costs, partially offset by savings in travel and expense (T&E). These adjustments reflect strategic priorities, including scaling marketing operations, advancing key technology initiatives, and supporting talent retention and development.  

While the forecast aligns with broader organizational goals, careful monitoring of risks, including vendor cost escalations and potential delays in project execution, will be critical to maintaining budget discipline.  

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **Marketing**: Total forecasted spend is $1,213,237, exceeding the budget by $33,399 (+2.8%). This increase reflects incremental investments in campaign activations and content development to support a seasonal push in Q3.  
- **People Costs**: Forecasted at $923,861, up $21,413 (+2.4%) from budget. The variance is tied to planned headcount additions in digital marketing and a mid-year adjustment to align compensation with market benchmarks.  
- **Professional Services**: Forecasted spend of $447,799 exceeds the budget by $12,624 (+2.9%). This increase is driven by expanded engagements with external agencies to support analytics and creative design for upcoming product launches.  
- **Technology**: At $312,751, forecasted spend is $14,543 (+4.9%) above budget. The variance reflects higher costs associated with renewals of key marketing platforms and new tools to enhance campaign performance tracking.  
- **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $143,814, representing a $4,659 (-3.1%) underspend. Reduced travel requirements for Q3 events and a shift toward virtual meetings have contributed to these savings.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
1. **Seasonal Campaigns**: Increased spend aligns with Q3 as a high-impact quarter for demand generation activities, including multi-channel campaigns and regional events.  
2. **Talent Investments**: Hiring for critical roles in digital marketing and analytics is expected to be completed by mid-Q3, driving incremental people costs.  
3. **Technology Renewals**: Renewals of key platforms, including marketing automation and customer data platforms, are scheduled for Q3, contributing to the technology variance.  
4. **Agency Support**: Expanded scope for agency partnerships to meet the demands of new product launches and market expansion initiatives.  

---

## **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Vendor Cost Increases**: Potential price escalations for technology renewals or agency contracts could further increase forecasted spend.  
2. **Project Delays**: Any delays in planned campaigns or hiring could shift costs
