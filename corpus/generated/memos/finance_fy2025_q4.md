### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
The Finance Department’s $890,883 favorable variance is attributable to the following factors:  

1. **Strategic Reorganization**: As part of Crestwood Capital Group’s ongoing transformation initiative, Finance operations were temporarily centralized under the Corporate Strategy Office. This consolidation resulted in a suspension of departmental activities, including hiring, vendor engagements, and project execution.  

2. **Headcount Freeze**: The $493,805 budget for People Costs remained unutilized due to a hiring freeze and the reassignment of existing personnel to other departments.  

3. **Vendor Engagements on Hold**: Professional Services ($176,353 budgeted) and Technology ($106,645 budgeted) expenses were eliminated as vendor contracts were renegotiated or deferred.  

4. **Reduced Operational Needs**: Travel & Expense ($44,223 budgeted) and Facilities & Occupancy ($69,856 budgeted) costs were avoided due to the shift to remote work and the suspension of in-person activities.  

While these variances are favorable in the short term, they reflect a deliberate pause rather than sustainable cost reductions.
