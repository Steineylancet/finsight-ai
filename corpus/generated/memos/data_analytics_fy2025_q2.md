## **Executive Summary**  
For FY2025 Q2, the Data & Analytics department incurred total expenses of $750,517, exceeding the budget of $518,655 by $231,863 (+44.7%). This variance was driven by higher-than-anticipated people costs, technology investments, and professional services. While these overages reflect strategic initiatives to scale our capabilities, they also highlight areas requiring tighter cost controls. The department remains focused on aligning future expenditures with budget expectations while maintaining operational excellence.

---

## **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs:** Actuals of $463,650 exceeded the budget of $312,182 by $151,468 (+48.5%).  
2. **Technology:** Actuals of $177,092 exceeded the budget of $129,385 by $47,708 (+36.9%).  
3. **Professional Services:** Actuals of $71,741 exceeded the budget of $51,104 by $20,638 (+40.4%).  
4. **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $38,034 exceeded the budget of $25,985 by $12,049 (+46.4%).  

Every category reported an unfavorable variance, with people costs contributing 65% of the total overage.

---

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
### **People Costs (+$151,468; +48.5%)**  
The significant increase in people costs was primarily due to a mid-quarter hiring surge to support the rollout of our enterprise-wide data modernization initiative. This included onboarding six senior data scientists and three analytics engineers, which temporarily inflated payroll and onboarding costs. Additionally, higher-than-expected health insurance premiums from key vendors (Aetna and Cigna) contributed to the variance.  

### **Technology (+$47,708; +36.9%)**  
Technology costs rose due to accelerated investments in cloud infrastructure and analytics platforms, including expanded licenses for Workday and Cornerstone OnDemand. These tools were critical to supporting the department’s growing headcount and ensuring seamless integration with new data pipelines.  

### **Professional Services (+$20,638; +40.4%)**  
The variance in professional services stemmed from unplanned consulting engagements with external data architects to address delays in the data warehouse migration project. These engagements were necessary to mitigate risks to project timelines.  

### **Travel & Expense (+$12,049; +46.4%)**  
Increased travel expenses reflect the department’s participation in multiple industry conferences and on-site vendor workshops. These activities were integral to knowledge sharing and vendor negotiations but exceeded initial allocations.  

---

## **Key Vendors**  
The top vendors for Q2 were primarily aligned with people costs and benefits administration:  
1. **Workday Inc ($483,383)** and **Cornerstone OnDemand ($458,846):** Both vendors supported HR and talent management systems, reflecting the increased headcount.  
2. **Principal Financial Group ($398,175), Aetna Health Insurance ($352,935), and Cigna Healthcare ($350,893):** These vendors provided employee benefits, with costs rising due to both headcount growth and higher premiums.
