### **Executive Summary**  
The Customer Success team in EMEA reported total expenses of $1,624,610 in FY2025 Q2, exceeding the budget of $1,129,920 by $494,690 (+43.8%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs, Technology investments, and Travel & Expense outlays. While these overages reflect strategic initiatives to bolster customer retention and growth, they warrant closer scrutiny to ensure alignment with long-term financial targets.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs:** Actuals of $884,099 surpassed the budget of $621,012 by $263,087 (+42.4%).  
2. **Technology:** Actuals of $255,530 exceeded the budget of $170,417 by $85,114 (+49.9%).  
3. **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $237,776 were $69,123 (+41.0%) over the budgeted $168,653.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy:** Actuals of $172,774 were $60,736 (+54.2%) above the budget of $112,038.  
5. **Professional Services:** Actuals of $74,432 exceeded the budget of $57,801 by $16,631 (+28.8%).  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+$263,087 / +42.4%)**  
   The increase in People Costs was largely driven by headcount expansion to support the onboarding of several high-value enterprise clients in the EMEA region. Additionally, investments in employee development through platforms such as Udemy Business ($450,683) and LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($535,479) contributed to the variance. These expenditures are aligned with our strategic focus on upskilling and retaining top talent, though they exceeded initial projections.  

2. **Technology (+$85,114 / +49.9%)**  
   Higher-than-expected spending on software licenses and integrations, particularly with Workday Inc. ($414,533) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($550,514), drove the unfavorable variance. These investments were necessary to enhance customer success analytics and automate workflows, but the timing of implementation accelerated costs into Q2.  

3. **Travel & Expense (+$69,123 / +41.0%)**  
   Increased travel expenses reflect a strategic push to engage with key clients in-person, particularly in high-growth markets such as Germany and the UAE. This initiative followed Q1 feedback indicating a preference for face-to-face interactions during onboarding and renewal discussions.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$60,736 / +54.2%)**  
   The variance in Facilities & Occupancy costs stems from the expansion of office space in London to accommodate the growing team. This decision supports our long-term growth strategy but contributed to short-term budget pressures.
