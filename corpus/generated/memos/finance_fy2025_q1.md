## Executive Summary  
For FY2025 Q1, the Finance Department incurred total expenses of $934,096, exceeding the budget of $666,233 by $267,862 (+40.2%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$149,866, +40.8%) and increased spending on Professional Services (+$45,730, +34.3%). While these variances reflect strategic investments in talent and operational enhancements, they also highlight the need for tighter cost controls in subsequent quarters.  

---

## Expense Performance by Category  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals totaled $517,038, exceeding the budget by $149,866 (+40.8%). This category represented 55% of total Q1 expenses.  
2. **Professional Services**: Actuals were $179,220, surpassing the budget by $45,730 (+34.3%).  
3. **Technology**: Spending reached $112,284, exceeding the budget by $32,474 (+40.7%).  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals were $79,939, over budget by $27,174 (+51.5%).  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Spending totaled $45,615, exceeding the budget by $12,618 (+38.2%).  

All categories experienced unfavorable variances, signaling a need for deeper analysis and potential corrective actions.  

---

## Key Variances & Commentary  
1. **People Costs (+$149,866, +40.8%)**: The variance was driven by higher-than-anticipated health insurance premiums and onboarding costs for new hires. MetLife Insurance accounted for $278,591, or 54% of total People Costs, reflecting increased coverage for new employees. Additionally, Workday Inc. ($122,726) and Indeed.com ($86,096) expenses were elevated due to recruitment system upgrades and job posting surges to fill critical roles.  

2. **Professional Services (+$45,730, +34.3%)**: The variance was primarily due to consulting fees for a compliance audit tied to new regulatory requirements. This was an unplanned but necessary expense to ensure adherence to evolving industry standards.  

3. **Technology (+$32,474, +40.7%)**: Higher spending was linked to the accelerated rollout of a new financial reporting platform. While this investment was not fully accounted for in the Q1 budget, it is expected to streamline reporting processes and reduce manual workloads in future quarters.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$27,174, +51.5%)**: Costs increased due to temporary office expansions to accommodate new hires and higher utility rates during the winter months.  

5. **Travel & Expense (+$12,618, +38.2%)**: Increased travel was tied to leadership’s participation in key industry conferences and client meetings, which were not fully anticipated during the budgeting process.
