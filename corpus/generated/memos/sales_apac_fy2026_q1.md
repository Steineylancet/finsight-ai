### **Executive Summary**  
The APAC Sales department closed FY2026 Q1 with total expenses of $30.91M, exceeding the budget of $27.88M by $3.04M (+10.9%). While revenue growth initiatives have been robust, cost containment remains a challenge. Key drivers of the variance include higher-than-expected People Costs (+44.4%), Travel & Expense (+42.9%), and Facilities & Occupancy (+46.8%). Renewals (+4.6%) and Consulting (+4.5%) were on track, reflecting disciplined management in these areas. Strategic investments in talent acquisition and client engagement contributed to the overspend, but these areas require closer monitoring to ensure alignment with long-term profitability goals.

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
- **People Costs:** Actuals reached $2.74M, exceeding the $1.90M budget by $843.8K (+44.4%).  
- **Renewals:** Delivered $14.15M, slightly above the $13.53M budget by $622.3K (+4.6%).  
- **New Business:** Closed at $6.07M, over the $5.66M budget by $403.5K (+7.1%).  
- **Travel & Expense:** Actuals of $1.07M exceeded the $746.9K budget by $320.7K (+42.9%).  
- **Professional Services:** Costs totaled $3.27M, $285.3K (+9.6%) higher than the $2.98M budget.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy:** Actuals of $533.5K exceeded the $363.3K budget by $170.2K (+46.8%).  
- **Technology:** Costs of $530.9K were $151.7K (+40.0%) above the $379.3K budget.  
- **Marketing:** Expenses of $523.4K exceeded the $372.0K budget by $151.4K (+40.7%).  
- **Consulting:** On track at $2.03M, $88.2K (+4.5%) above the $1.94M budget.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+44.4%, +$843.8K):**  
   The variance reflects an accelerated hiring plan to support growth in high-potential markets, particularly in Southeast Asia. Top vendors, including Aetna Health Insurance ($1.15M) and MetLife Insurance ($904.8K), contributed to increased benefits costs for new hires. Additionally, investments in LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($902.2K) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($772.8K) for recruitment and training tools drove costs higher than anticipated.

2. **Travel & Expense (+42.9%, +$320.7K):**  
   Increased client engagement activities, including in-person meetings and regional conferences, drove higher travel costs. This reflects a strategic push to strengthen relationships in key markets like China and India, where face-to-face interactions remain critical.

3. **
