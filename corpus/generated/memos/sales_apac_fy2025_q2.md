### **Executive Summary**  
In FY2025 Q2, the APAC Sales department reported total expenses of $35.1M, exceeding the budget of $32.6M by $2.46M (+7.5%). Key drivers of this variance include elevated People Costs (+37.6%), Travel & Expense (+40.8%), and Facilities & Occupancy (+45.3%). While Renewals (+2.0%) and New Business (+4.7%) expenses remained on track, the overall unfavorable variance reflects strategic investments in talent acquisition, market expansion, and operational scaling. These investments align with the department’s long-term growth objectives but warrant ongoing monitoring to ensure cost discipline.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
- **People Costs:** Actuals reached $2.81M, exceeding the $2.04M budget by $766.6K (+37.6%).  
- **Travel & Expense:** Expenses totaled $1.15M, $332.0K (+40.8%) above the $813.1K budget.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy:** Actuals of $585.0K surpassed the $402.6K budget by $182.3K (+45.3%).  
- **Technology:** Costs of $569.9K exceeded the $396.3K budget by $173.6K (+43.8%).  
- **Marketing:** Actuals of $569.3K were $162.8K (+40.1%) over the $406.4K budget.  
- **New Business, Renewals, Professional Services, and Consulting:** All remained within acceptable thresholds, with variances ranging from +2.0% to +4.7%.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+37.6% / +$766.6K):**  
   The variance reflects accelerated hiring to support APAC’s aggressive growth strategy, particularly in high-growth markets like Southeast Asia. Additionally, higher-than-anticipated onboarding costs and increased reliance on premium health insurance providers (e.g., Aetna Health Insurance at $1.12M and Principal Financial Group at $1.02M) contributed to the overrun.  

2. **Travel & Expense (+40.8% / +$332.0K):**  
   Increased client-facing activities, including roadshows and in-person deal negotiations, drove higher travel costs. This surge aligns with the post-pandemic recovery of in-person engagements in key markets such as Japan and Australia.  

3. **Facilities & Occupancy (+45.3% / +$182.3K):**  
   The variance stems from the opening of two satellite offices in Singapore and Vietnam to support regional expansion. These facilities were not fully accounted for in the original budget.  

4. **Technology (+43.8% / +$173.6K):**  
   Investments in sales enablement tools, including expanded licenses for LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($734.3K) and Udemy Business ($830.7K), drove the overrun. These tools aim to enhance team productivity and upskill employees.
