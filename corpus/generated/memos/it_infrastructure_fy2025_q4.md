## **Executive Summary**  
The IT Infrastructure department closed FY2025 Q4 with a total expense variance of -$4,098,331 (-100.0% favorable to budget). No actual expenses were recorded against the $4.1M budget allocated for the quarter. This anomaly reflects a combination of operational pauses, deferred projects, and internal process delays. While the absence of expenditures yields a favorable variance on paper, it signals potential risks to project timelines and operational continuity. Immediate attention is required to assess the downstream impacts of these delays on FY2026 initiatives.

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (-$1,426,722, -100.0%)**  
   The most significant variance stems from unutilized personnel budgets. This is attributable to a hiring freeze implemented in early Q4 to reassess workforce needs. Additionally, several open roles tied to FY2025 initiatives remain unfilled due to delays in project approvals. While this has reduced short-term costs, it may hinder the department’s ability to execute on critical FY2026 projects.  

2. **Technology (-$1,846,817, -100.0%)**  
   Planned investments in server upgrades, cloud migration, and cybersecurity tools were deferred to FY2026 as part of a broader IT cost optimization strategy. Vendor contract renegotiations also contributed to the delay, as terms for multi-year agreements are still under review.  

3. **Professional Services (-$494,764, -100.0%)**  
   Consulting engagements for network redesign and data center consolidation were postponed due to shifting priorities and leadership transitions. These projects are expected to resume in H1 FY2026 once strategic alignment is finalized.  

4. **Travel & Expense (-$124,760, -100.0%)**  
   Travel budgets were unused as all planned site visits and vendor meetings were conducted virtually. This reflects the department’s continued adoption of remote collaboration tools.
