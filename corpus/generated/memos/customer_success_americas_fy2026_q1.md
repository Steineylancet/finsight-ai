### **Executive Summary**  
The Customer Success – Americas department reported total expenses of $2,944,770 in FY2026 Q1, exceeding the budget of $2,034,312 by $910,457 (+44.8%). This unfavorable variance was driven by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$526,680, +47.1%) and elevated spending across Travel & Expense, Technology, and Facilities categories. These increases reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition, onboarding, and customer engagement initiatives, though they also highlight areas requiring tighter cost control.  

While the department's focus on scaling operations to support customer retention and growth is commendable, the variance underscores the need for improved forecasting and expense management as we progress through the fiscal year.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  

1. **People Costs**: Actuals were $1,644,291, exceeding the budget of $1,117,611 by $526,680 (+47.1%).  
2. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals reached $435,856, up $128,545 (+41.8%) from the budgeted $307,311.  
3. **Technology**: Spending totaled $430,120, surpassing the budget of $306,446 by $123,674 (+40.4%).  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals were $293,631, exceeding the $202,115 budget by $91,516 (+45.3%).  
5. **Professional Services**: Actuals came in at $140,873, $40,043 (+39.7%) over the $100,830 budget.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  

1. **People Costs (+$526,680, +47.1%)**  
   The variance is primarily attributed to accelerated hiring efforts to support a 15% increase in customer accounts across the Americas. LinkedIn Talent Solutions ($542,148) and Indeed.com ($399,554) were heavily utilized to source talent, while onboarding and training costs were higher than anticipated. Additionally, ADP Payroll Services ($546,182) and Aetna Health Insurance ($535,613) reflect increased headcount and expanded benefits coverage.

2. **Travel & Expense (+$128,545, +41.8%)**  
   The department ramped up in-person customer visits to strengthen relationships and address retention challenges. This included travel to key accounts in high-growth regions such as South America. Seasonal factors, including Q1 kickoff meetings and team-building events, also contributed to the overage.

3. **Technology (+$123,674, +40.4%)**  
   Investments in customer success tools, such as Cornerstone OnDemand ($474,800), were higher than planned due to the accelerated rollout of training and performance management platforms. These tools are critical to improving team productivity and customer satisfaction but require better alignment with budget cycles.

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$91,516, +45.3%)**  
   Office expansion in high-growth markets, including Brazil and Mexico, drove this variance. Additionally, temporary coworking spaces were utilized to accommodate new hires during the onboarding surge.
