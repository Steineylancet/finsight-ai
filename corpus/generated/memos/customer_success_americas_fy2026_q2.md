### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q2, the Customer Success - Americas department is forecasted to exceed its budget by $51,785 (+2.2%), driven primarily by higher-than-anticipated People Costs. Total forecasted spend is $2,413,301 compared to a budget of $2,361,517. While some categories, such as Technology and Travel & Expense, are expected to come in slightly under budget, increased investments in personnel and professional services are driving the overall variance. This outlook reflects strategic decisions to support growth initiatives and maintain service excellence, but careful monitoring of discretionary spending will be necessary to manage risks.

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
- **People Costs:** Forecasted at $1,360,277 (+$63,473, +4.9% vs. budget). This increase reflects planned hiring to support customer retention initiatives and onboarding efforts for new accounts. Additionally, merit-based salary adjustments and higher-than-expected benefit costs are contributing to the variance.  
- **Technology:** Forecasted at $354,624 (-$1,606, -0.5% vs. budget). Savings are attributed to favorable pricing on software renewals and deferred implementation of certain non-critical tools.  
- **Travel & Expense:** Forecasted at $348,401 (-$6,459, -1.8% vs. budget). Reduced travel activity, particularly for internal team meetings, is driving this variance. However, travel for customer-facing engagements remains a priority.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy:** Forecasted at $228,619 (-$7,212, -3.1% vs. budget). This reflects ongoing optimization of office space utilization and renegotiated lease terms in select locations.  
- **Professional Services:** Forecasted at $121,380 (+$3,588, +3.0% vs. budget). The increase is tied to consulting support for process improvement projects and an expanded scope for a key vendor partnership.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
1. **Headcount Growth:** The forecast assumes the addition of five new hires in Q2 to address increased customer demand and improve response times.  
2. **Seasonal Trends:** Q2 typically sees higher customer onboarding activity, necessitating additional professional services and training efforts.  
3. **Cost Optimization:** Savings in Technology and Facilities are expected to partially offset higher People Costs and Professional Services.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Hiring Timelines:** Delays in planned hiring could impact the ability to meet customer satisfaction targets.  
2. **Vendor Dependence:** Increased reliance on professional services vendors introduces the risk of cost overruns if project scopes expand further.  
3. **Travel Resurgence:** While travel spend is forecasted under budget, any unplanned customer escalations or in-person engagements could drive costs higher.  

---

The FP&A team will continue to monitor spending trends
