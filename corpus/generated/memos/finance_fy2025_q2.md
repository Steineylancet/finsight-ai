### **Executive Summary**  
In FY2025 Q2, the Finance Department recorded total expenses of $1,131,667, exceeding the budget of $761,497 by $370,170 (+48.6%). This unfavorable variance was driven by increased People Costs (+$221,982, +53.1%) and higher-than-anticipated spending across all other categories. Key drivers include unplanned headcount growth, expanded use of professional services for strategic initiatives, and technology investments to support operational scalability. While these costs reflect necessary investments in talent and infrastructure, they highlight the need for tighter cost controls in subsequent quarters.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals of $639,828 exceeded the budget of $417,846 by $221,982 (+53.1%).  
2. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $222,420 exceeded the budget of $151,425 by $70,995 (+46.9%).  
3. **Technology**: Actuals of $123,690 exceeded the budget of $93,255 by $30,435 (+32.6%).  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals of $90,302 exceeded the budget of $60,359 by $29,943 (+49.6%).  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $55,428 exceeded the budget of $38,613 by $16,815 (+43.5%).  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+$221,982, +53.1%)**:  
   The largest variance stems from accelerated hiring to support the company’s growth initiatives, particularly in risk management and compliance. Additionally, increased reliance on recruitment platforms like Indeed.com ($198,467) and higher health insurance premiums from Cigna Healthcare ($177,808) and Aetna Health Insurance ($130,582) contributed to the overage.  

2. **Professional Services (+$70,995, +46.9%)**:  
   The variance reflects unbudgeted consulting engagements to support the implementation of new regulatory reporting requirements and a mid-quarter audit of internal controls. These initiatives were critical to maintaining compliance and operational integrity.  

3. **Technology (+$30,435, +32.6%)**:  
   Investments in Workday Inc. ($81,087) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($93,493) drove the overage, as we fast-tracked upgrades to our HR and financial systems to accommodate increased headcount and improve data analytics capabilities.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$29,943, +49.6%)**:  
   Office expansion to support new hires and higher utility costs due to seasonal usage patterns caused this variance.  

5. **Travel & Expense (+$16,815, +43.5%)**:  
   Increased travel for leadership to attend industry conferences and client meetings drove costs higher than anticipated.
