## **Executive Summary**  
In FY2025 Q2, the Human Resources (HR) department reported total expenses of $833,776, exceeding the budget of $571,668 by $262,109 (+45.8%). This unfavorable variance was driven primarily by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$139,340, +49.0%) and increased spending across Technology, Professional Services, Travel & Expense, and Facilities & Occupancy categories. These variances reflect strategic investments in workforce expansion, technology upgrades, and employee engagement initiatives. While these expenditures align with long-term growth objectives, tighter cost controls and improved forecasting will be critical in the coming quarters.  

---

## **Expense Performance by Category**  

1. **People Costs**: Actuals of $423,665 exceeded the budget of $284,324 by $139,340 (+49.0%). This category accounted for 51% of the total variance.

4. **Travel & Expense**: Expenses amounted to $83,931, compared to a budget of $57,752, resulting in a $26,180 variance (+45.3%).

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  

### **People Costs (+$139,340, +49.0%)**  
The variance in People Costs reflects a 12% increase in headcount during Q2 to support the company’s strategic growth initiatives, particularly in talent acquisition and employee development. Notable contributors include higher payroll processing fees through ADP Payroll Services ($116,479) and expanded use of Udemy Business ($74,408) for employee training programs. Additionally, rising healthcare premiums through MetLife Insurance ($59,019) and Cigna Healthcare ($48,236) contributed to the variance.  

### **Technology (+$37,949, +45.0%)**  
The HR department implemented new modules in Workday Inc. ($65,918) to enhance workforce analytics and compliance reporting. This unbudgeted investment was necessary to address scalability concerns as the company grows.  

### **Professional Services (+$33,757, +38.7%)**  
Higher-than-expected consulting fees were incurred for a comprehensive compensation benchmarking study. This initiative aims to align our total rewards strategy with market trends, supporting employee retention in a competitive labor market.  

### **Travel & Expense (+$26,180, +45.3%)**  
Travel costs increased due to a series of in-person recruitment events and leadership training sessions held across multiple regions. These activities are part of a broader effort to strengthen our employer brand and leadership pipeline.
