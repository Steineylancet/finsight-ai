## **Executive Summary**  
The Sales - Americas department reported total expenses of $49.9M in FY2025 Q3, exceeding the budget of $45.7M by $4.2M (+9.2%). Key drivers of the unfavorable variance include higher-than-anticipated People Costs (+$923.8K, +43.6%) and Renewals-related expenses (+$2.1M, +8.9%). These increases reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition, retention initiatives, and expanded customer engagement efforts. While these expenditures are above plan, they align with revenue growth priorities and long-term market positioning.  

## **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **Renewals**: Actuals of $25.3M exceeded the $23.3M budget by $2.1M (+8.9%).  
2. **People Costs**: Actuals of $3.0M exceeded the $2.1M budget by $923.8K (+43.6%).  
3. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $1.2M exceeded the $830.1K budget by $387.2K (+46.6%).  
4. **Technology**: Actuals of $600.2K exceeded the $413.5K budget by $186.7K (+45.1%).  
5. **Marketing**: Actuals of $590.9K exceeded the $409.4K budget by $181.5K (+44.3%).  
6. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals of $587.9K exceeded the $411.7K budget by $176.3K (+42.8%).  
7. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $5.2M slightly exceeded the $5.1M budget by $130.3K (+2.5%).  
8. **Consulting**: Actuals of $3.4M slightly exceeded the $3.3M budget by $128.7K (+3.9%).  
9. **New Business**: Actuals of $9.9M were on track with the $9.8M budget (+0.5%).  

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
- **Renewals (+$2.1M, +8.9%)**: The variance reflects increased customer retention efforts, including expanded contract renegotiation teams and higher incentive payouts for early renewals. This aligns with our strategy to secure long-term revenue stability.  
- **People Costs (+$923.8K, +43.6%)**: Elevated costs are attributed to headcount growth to support new business initiatives, as well as higher benefits expenses. Notably, top vendors such as Cigna Healthcare ($1.4M) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($1.3M) contributed to this variance.  
- **Travel & Expense (+$387.2K, +46.6%)**: Increased travel reflects intensified in-person client engagement and participation in industry events, a strategic pivot to deepen customer relationships post-pandemic.
