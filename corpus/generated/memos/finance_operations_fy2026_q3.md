## Executive Summary  
The Finance Operations department is projected to close FY2026 Q3 with total expenses of $1,800,758, exceeding the budget of $1,750,443 by $50,314 (+2.9%). Key drivers of this variance include higher-than-anticipated People Costs, Professional Services, and Facilities & Occupancy expenses. While Travel & Expense is forecasted to come in slightly below budget (-2.2%), this reduction is insufficient to offset the increases in other categories.  

This forecast reflects strategic investments in technology and professional services to support ongoing process improvements, as well as adjustments for inflationary pressures in facilities costs. While the variance is manageable, close monitoring of discretionary expenses and vendor negotiations will be critical to mitigating risks in the latter half of the fiscal year.  

---

## Planned Spend by Category  

1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $984,202, exceeding budget by $19,501 (+2.0%). This increase reflects planned hiring for two key roles in compliance and reporting, as well as higher-than-expected merit adjustments for existing staff.  

2. **Professional Services**: Projected at $366,994, surpassing budget by $19,895 (+5.7%). The variance is driven by consulting engagements to support the implementation of the new financial reporting platform and advisory services related to regulatory compliance.  

3. **Technology**: Expected to total $213,422, slightly above budget by $2,117 (+1.0%). This reflects incremental costs for software license renewals and cloud storage capacity to accommodate growing data volumes.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $149,787, exceeding budget by $10,740 (+7.7%). The increase is attributed to rising utility costs and a mid-year lease escalation for office space.  

5. **Travel & Expense**: Anticipated at $86,353, coming in under budget by $1,938 (-2.2%). Reduced travel activity, particularly for regional team meetings, is driving this favorable variance.  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  

- **Planned Hiring**: Two new hires in compliance and reporting are expected to onboard mid-quarter, contributing to higher People Costs.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals of key software licenses and incremental storage needs are factored into the Technology forecast.  
- **Consulting Projects**: Professional Services costs reflect ongoing work on the financial reporting platform and regulatory advisory support.  
- **Seasonal Trends**: Travel & Expense forecasts assume continued moderation in travel activity, consistent with recent seasonal patterns.
