### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (-$357,091; -100.0%)**  
   The largest variance stems from deferred headcount additions. Several planned hires were postponed due to a strategic decision to reassess the department’s resource needs in light of shifting priorities. Additionally, attrition in Q3 was not backfilled as originally anticipated. While this decision reduced short-term costs, it may delay progress on key analytics initiatives in FY2026.  

2. **Technology (-$152,858; -100.0%)**  
   Technology expenses were significantly below budget due to the postponement of a planned data infrastructure upgrade. This delay was driven by ongoing vendor negotiations to secure more favorable terms for a multi-year contract. The project is now expected to commence in FY2026 Q2, with incremental costs anticipated in future quarters.  

3. **Professional Services (-$61,815; -100.0%)**  
   No professional services expenses were incurred this quarter as the department opted to leverage internal resources for ongoing projects. While this approach delivered cost savings, it may have stretched existing team capacity, potentially impacting project timelines.  

4. **Travel & Expense (-$29,657; -100.0%)**  
   Travel expenses were entirely eliminated this quarter due to a continued focus on virtual collaboration. This reflects a broader company-wide trend of reducing discretionary travel to align with cost containment goals.

### **Key Vendors**  
No transactions were recorded with vendors this quarter, as major projects requiring external support were deferred. However, ongoing discussions with key technology and consulting partners suggest a ramp-up in vendor engagement in FY2026.
