## Executive Summary  
The Data & Analytics department is projected to close FY2026 Q2 with total expenses of $561,215, reflecting a modest forecasted variance of +$1,612 (+0.3%) above the approved budget of $559,603. This variance is primarily driven by higher-than-expected costs in Professional Services and Travel & Expense, partially offset by slight underspend in People Costs and Technology. The outlook aligns closely with the department’s operational priorities, including ongoing investments in talent, technology infrastructure, and external consulting to support strategic data initiatives.

---

## Planned Spend by Category  
- **People Costs:** Forecasted at $336,075, slightly under budget by $2,241 (-0.7%). This reflects the timing of planned hires, with one senior data engineer now expected to onboard in early Q3 rather than Q2.  
- **Technology:** Forecasted at $137,576, underspending by $1,052 (-0.8%). The variance is attributed to favorable pricing on a key software renewal, which was negotiated below initial budget assumptions.  
- **Professional Services:** Forecasted at $59,347, exceeding budget by $4,674 (+8.5%). The increase is driven by expanded scope for an external consulting engagement to support the rollout of a new machine learning platform.  
- **Travel & Expense:** Forecasted at $28,216, slightly over budget by $230 (+0.8%). This reflects anticipated travel for the department’s participation in an industry conference and on-site workshops with external vendors.  

---

## Forecast Assumptions & Drivers  
1. **Hiring & Talent Investments:** The forecast assumes a slight delay in planned hiring for a senior data engineer, impacting People Costs in Q2. However, the team remains on track to meet its FY2026 headcount goals.  
2. **Technology Renewals:** Savings on a key software license renewal contributed to the favorable Technology variance, while other planned technology investments remain on schedule.  
3. **Consulting Engagements:** A decision to expand the scope of a consulting project to accelerate the deployment of advanced analytics tools is the primary driver of the Professional Services variance.  
4. **Seasonal Travel Needs:** Increased travel activity in Q2 aligns with the department’s participation in critical industry events and vendor collaboration sessions.  

---

## Risks & Watch Items  
1. **Consulting Costs:** The expanded consulting engagement introduces a risk of further cost overruns if additional scope changes are required. Close monitoring will be essential to mitigate this risk.  
2. **Hiring Timeline:** Any further delays in planned hiring could impact the department’s ability to execute key initiatives in the second half of FY2026.  
3. **Technology Dependencies:** While software renewals have been favorable, any delays in vendor delivery or technical implementation could affect project timelines and future quarters’ budgets.  

---

The Data & Analytics department remains well-positioned to execute its strategic priorities in FY2026 Q2 within a tightly managed budget
