### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
The department’s 100% favorable variance across all expense categories reflects a deliberate operational pause during the quarter. Key factors include:  

- **Deferred Hiring and Workforce Optimization:** The $710,134 variance in People Costs is attributed to a hiring freeze initiated in Q3 and the temporary reassignment of existing staff to other regions. This measure aligns with the broader company objective of optimizing headcount in response to shifting market demands.  
- **Postponed Initiatives:** Professional Services ($64,321) and Technology ($192,410) budgets were allocated for customer onboarding automation and CRM enhancements. These projects were deferred to FY2026 as part of the restructuring plan.  
- **Travel Freeze:** The $194,167 variance in Travel & Expense reflects the suspension of in-person client engagements. Virtual meetings were leveraged instead, with no measurable impact on customer satisfaction metrics.  
- **Facilities Cost Avoidance:** The $129,133 variance in Facilities & Occupancy stems from the temporary closure of regional offices, with employees working remotely or redeployed to other regions.  

These variances are consistent with the department’s temporary operational pause and do not reflect a long-term reduction in resource requirements.
