### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q4, the Executive Department's forecasted total spend is $2,601,208, representing a 1.7% increase (+$43,909) over the budgeted amount of $2,557,299. The primary drivers of this variance are incremental increases in People Costs, Travel & Expense, and Insurance & Risk, partially offset by a reduction in Professional Services. This outlook reflects strategic investments in leadership initiatives, travel related to business development, and updated insurance premiums, while maintaining control over discretionary spend.

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $1,552,310, exceeding budget by $14,975 (+1.0%). This increase reflects planned executive hires in Q4 to support strategic growth initiatives, as well as adjustments for merit-based salary increases and year-end performance bonuses.  

2. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $476,229, up $13,435 (+2.9%) from budget. This increase is driven by anticipated travel for leadership to attend international investor meetings and industry conferences, aligning with Crestwood’s global expansion strategy.  

3. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $249,296, under budget by $6,663 (-2.6%). This reflects a reduction in reliance on external consultants due to the completion of key strategic advisory projects in Q3.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $186,428, exceeding budget by $9,678 (+5.5%). The variance is attributed to higher-than-expected costs for office maintenance and utilities, as well as upgrades to executive meeting spaces to accommodate hybrid work needs.  

5. **Insurance & Risk**: Forecasted at $136,944, up $12,484 (+10.0%). The increase reflects updated premiums following the annual policy renewal process, which incorporated higher coverage limits to mitigate evolving operational risks.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Strategic Headcount Growth**: The department plans to onboard two senior executives in Q4, driving the increase in People Costs.  
- **Increased Travel Activity**: Travel spend is expected to rise due to expanded business development efforts in key international markets.  
- **Cost Containment in Professional Services**: With major consulting projects concluding in Q3, reliance on external advisory services will decline.  
- **Insurance Adjustments**: Updated premiums reflect Crestwood’s proactive risk management approach and expanded coverage requirements.  

---

### **Risks & Watch Items**  
1. **Macroeconomic Volatility**: Changes in economic conditions could impact travel costs and insurance premiums.  
2. **Hiring Delays**: Any delays in planned executive hires may reduce People Costs but could hinder strategic initiatives.  
3. **Unanticipated Facilities Costs**: Potential unforeseen expenses related to office maintenance or technology upgrades could further pressure Facilities & Occupancy spend.
