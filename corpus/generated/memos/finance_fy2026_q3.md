## **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q3, Crestwood Capital Group’s Finance Department is forecasting total expenses of $803,871, exceeding the budget of $783,882 by $19,989 (+2.6%). This variance is primarily driven by incremental investments in People Costs, Technology, and Professional Services, which align with our strategic initiatives for operational scalability and efficiency. While the forecast reflects modest increases across all categories, these are necessary to support planned growth, enhance systems infrastructure, and maintain operational continuity.

---

## **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $438,153, exceeding the budget by $5,539 (+1.3%). This increase reflects planned hiring to support new regulatory reporting requirements and backfilling critical roles.  
2. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $160,835, $5,322 (+3.4%) above budget. The variance is driven by consulting engagements for process automation and compliance-related advisory services.  
3. **Technology**: Forecasted at $99,755, $6,452 (+6.9%) above budget. The increase is attributed to software license renewals and expanded cloud storage capacity to support data growth.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $63,399, slightly exceeding the budget by $373 (+0.6%). This reflects minor adjustments for inflationary increases in office-related costs.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $41,728, $2,304 (+5.8%) above budget. The variance is driven by higher-than-anticipated travel activity for leadership offsites and vendor negotiations.  

---

## **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Strategic Hiring**: The forecast assumes two additional hires in Q3 to address workload demands in regulatory compliance and financial planning.  
- **Technology Investments**: Renewals for key financial systems and additional cloud services are expected to drive Technology costs higher. These investments are critical to maintaining operational resilience.  
- **Increased Vendor Engagement**: Professional Services costs reflect ongoing automation initiatives and external expertise to refine internal processes.  
- **Seasonal Travel Uptick**: Travel & Expense assumes increased travel for Q3 due to planned leadership strategy sessions and vendor site visits.  

---

## **Risks & Watch Items**  
- **Hiring Delays**: Any delays in onboarding planned hires could reduce People Costs but may strain existing resources.  
- **Technology Cost Escalation**: Vendor pricing adjustments for software renewals could further impact Technology spend.  
- **Professional Services Scope Creep**: Unanticipated project scope changes could elevate consulting costs beyond forecasted levels.  
- **Macroeconomic Pressures**: Inflationary trends could impact Facilities & Occupancy costs more significantly than anticipated.  

---
