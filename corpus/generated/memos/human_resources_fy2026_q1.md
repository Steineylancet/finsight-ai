## Executive Summary  
In FY2026 Q1, the Human Resources (HR) department reported total expenses of $775,398, exceeding the budget of $531,660 by $243,739 (+45.8%). This unfavorable variance was driven by higher-than-anticipated costs across all major categories, particularly People Costs (+$119,357, +44.8%) and Professional Services (+$41,842, +52.5%). Key drivers include increased headcount initiatives, expanded learning and development efforts, and higher-than-expected reliance on external vendors for HR technology and benefits administration. While these investments align with strategic priorities, cost control measures will be critical in subsequent quarters to mitigate budgetary pressures.

---

## Expense Performance by Category  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals of $386,031 exceeded the budget of $266,674 by $119,357 (+44.8%). This category represents 49.8% of total HR spending this quarter.  
2. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $121,484 exceeded the budget of $79,641 by $41,842 (+52.5%), reflecting increased reliance on external consultants to support HR transformation projects.  
3. **Technology**: Actuals of $116,294 exceeded the budget of $80,576 by $35,718 (+44.3%), largely due to higher-than-expected software licensing fees.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals of $76,317 exceeded the budget of $52,868 by $23,449 (+44.4%), driven by onboarding-related facility usage and office expansions.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $75,273 exceeded the budget of $51,901 by $23,372 (+45.0%), reflecting increased travel for recruitment events and leadership training sessions.  

---

## Key Variances & Commentary  
- **People Costs**: The $119,357 variance was primarily driven by increased headcount. HR onboarded 15 new hires across departments, exceeding initial projections, and incurred higher benefits costs with vendors such as Cigna Healthcare ($49,907) and Principal Financial Group ($51,039). Additionally, expanded learning initiatives, including Udemy Business ($47,275), contributed to the variance.  
- **Professional Services**: The $41,842 variance reflects consulting fees for Workday Inc. ($52,065) to support the ongoing HRIS optimization project. This initiative aims to streamline payroll and performance management processes but required more resources than initially scoped.  
- **Technology**: The $35,718 variance was driven by higher-than-anticipated costs for Cornerstone OnDemand ($96,879), which supported the rollout of a new talent management platform.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy**: The $23,449 variance reflects increased onboarding-related facility usage and temporary office space for new hires.  
- **Travel & Expense**: The $23,372 variance was driven by higher travel activity for recruitment fairs and leadership development programs, which were reinstated after pandemic-related pauses.
