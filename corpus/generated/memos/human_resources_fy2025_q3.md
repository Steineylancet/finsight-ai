### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2025 Q3, the Human Resources department incurred total expenses of $765,254, exceeding the budget of $527,526 by $237,728 (+45.1%). Variances were observed across all expense categories, driven by increased headcount, strategic investments in technology, and higher-than-anticipated professional services and travel costs. While these investments align with our long-term talent and operational strategies, they underscore the need for tighter cost management and improved forecasting processes in future quarters.

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  

1. **People Costs (+$116,663, +45.0%)**  
   The largest variance stems from People Costs, driven by a 12% increase in headcount during Q3 to support the ongoing expansion of our Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives and the launch of the Leadership Development Program. Additionally, vendor costs from ADP Payroll Services ($103,545) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($74,337) reflect higher usage of payroll and learning management systems due to onboarding activities and training sessions.  

2. **Technology (+$39,011, +48.7%)**  
   Investments in HR technology exceeded budget due to the accelerated implementation of a new performance management module within Cornerstone OnDemand. This unplanned but strategic decision was made to address gaps identified in mid-year employee engagement surveys.  

3. **Professional Services (+$31,528, +39.2%)**  
   Higher-than-expected expenditures in Professional Services were driven by consulting fees for an external compensation benchmarking study and legal advisory services related to updated labor compliance requirements in multiple jurisdictions.  

4. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$26,038, +48.1%)**  
   Facilities costs increased due to additional office space leased to accommodate new hires and the temporary use of off-site venues for team-building workshops.  

5. **Travel & Expense (+$24,489, +45.8%)**  
   Travel expenses spiked due to increased participation in industry conferences and regional leadership summits, which were reinstated this quarter after several years of virtual-only events.
