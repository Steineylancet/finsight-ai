### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2026 Q3, IT Infrastructure is forecasted to exceed its budget of $3,635,453 by $46,468 (+1.3%), with total forecasted spend of $3,681,921. The primary drivers of this variance are increased People Costs (+$34,000, +2.7%) and minor overages in Technology (+$6,676, +0.4%) and Professional Services (+$6,444, +1.5%). These are partially offset by a modest reduction in Travel & Expense (-$1,855, -1.7%). The forecast reflects anticipated investments in key initiatives, including cloud infrastructure upgrades, vendor contract renewals, and planned headcount expansion.  

---

### **Planned Spend by Category**  
1. **Technology**: Forecasted at $1,634,237, slightly above budget (+$6,676, +0.4%). This increase reflects planned software license renewals and incremental cloud storage capacity to support ongoing scalability efforts.  
2. **People Costs**: Forecasted at $1,312,813, exceeding budget by $34,000 (+2.7%). This variance is driven by the onboarding of two additional network engineers in Q3 to support the enterprise-wide data center modernization project.  
3. **Professional Services**: Forecasted at $444,710, up $6,444 (+1.5%) due to consulting fees for the cybersecurity audit and external support for the migration to a new IT service management platform.  
4. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Forecasted at $181,736, marginally over budget (+$1,203, +0.7%) due to increased utility costs for data center operations.  
5. **Travel & Expense**: Forecasted at $108,426, under budget by $1,855 (-1.7%). This reflects lower-than-expected travel needs for vendor site visits, as more meetings are conducted virtually.  

---

### **Forecast Assumptions & Drivers**  
- **Headcount Expansion**: Two additional hires in Q3 are expected to address resource constraints in network operations.  
- **Cloud Investments**: Continued migration to cloud infrastructure necessitates incremental storage and computing capacity.  
- **Project-Driven Spend**: Key initiatives, such as the cybersecurity audit and IT service platform migration, require external expertise, driving up Professional Services costs.  
- **Seasonality**: Q3 typically sees higher spend due to mid-year contract renewals and project ramp-ups.  

---

2. **Recruitment Delays**: Delays in onboarding planned hires could shift People Costs into Q4, potentially impacting project timelines.
