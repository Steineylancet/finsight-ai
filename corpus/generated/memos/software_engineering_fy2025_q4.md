### **Executive Summary**  
The Software Engineering department reported $0 in actual expenses for FY2025 Q4 against a budget of $9,082,045, resulting in a 100% favorable variance across all categories. This outcome reflects a deliberate pause in departmental spending due to the strategic transition of engineering functions to an outsourced model. The variance aligns with management’s decision to defer expenditures while finalizing vendor contracts and restructuring internal operations. This temporary suspension of spend is expected to normalize in FY2026 Q1 as new vendor agreements are executed and operational activities resume.

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **Facilities & Occupancy (-$182,560 / -100%)**: The department did not incur any facilities-related costs this quarter due to the planned consolidation of engineering teams into shared coworking spaces. This initiative, originally slated for Q4, was deferred to FY2026 Q1 pending lease renegotiations.  

2. **Travel & Expense (-$459,545 / -100%)**: Travel budgets were unused as all in-person engineering summits were postponed. This decision reflects a shift to virtual collaboration tools during the vendor transition period.  

3. **Professional Services (-$713,155 / -100%)**: The variance stems from delayed onboarding of external consultants for the cloud migration project. Contract negotiations with key vendors are ongoing and expected to close early next quarter.  

4. **Technology (-$1,826,473 / -100%)**: Planned investments in software licenses and infrastructure upgrades were deferred due to the department’s transition to a fully outsourced development model. These costs will be reflected in vendor payments starting FY2026 Q1.  

5. **People Costs (-$5,900,311 / -100%)**: The department’s headcount reduction, completed in FY2025 Q3, eliminated salary and benefits expenses in Q4. Remaining personnel were reassigned to other departments or transitioned to vendor roles.
