### **Executive Summary**  
For FY2025 Q1, the Executive Department reported total expenses of $2,529,138, exceeding the budget of $1,800,663 by $728,475 (+40.5%). This unfavorable variance was driven by higher-than-expected People Costs, Travel & Expense, and Professional Services, reflecting strategic investments in talent acquisition, leadership initiatives, and increased executive travel. While these expenditures align with key growth priorities, they require careful monitoring to ensure alignment with annual financial targets.

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  

1. **People Costs (+$414,352, +38.1%)**  
   The largest variance stems from People Costs, driven by several factors:  
   - **Headcount Expansion:** Strategic hiring for senior leadership roles and support staff resulted in increased payroll expenses. ADP Payroll Services and Workday Inc. accounted for $287,537 and $407,841, respectively.  
   - **Recruitment Efforts:** Investments in talent acquisition platforms, such as Indeed.com ($292,096), supported aggressive hiring goals for key growth initiatives.  
   - **Benefits Costs:** Higher-than-anticipated healthcare premiums with Cigna Healthcare ($279,916) and life insurance coverage through MetLife Insurance ($429,617) also contributed to the variance.  

2. **Travel & Expense (+$157,825, +49.8%)**  
   Executive travel increased significantly due to expanded participation in industry conferences, client meetings, and investor roadshows. This reflects a deliberate effort to strengthen external relationships and promote Crestwood Capital’s strategic initiatives.  

3. **Professional Services (+$63,394, +34.9%)**  
   The variance in Professional Services is attributed to consulting engagements for leadership development programs and strategic advisory projects initiated during the quarter.
