### **Executive Summary**  
The EMEA Sales department closed FY2025 Q4 with total expenses of $35.85M, coming in $482.6K (1.3%) under the budgeted $36.33M. While overall expense management was favorable, notable overspending occurred in Renewals (+12.5%) and Professional Services (+6.5%). These were offset by significant cost savings in Marketing, Technology, Facilities, Travel, and People Costs, all of which reported zero spend due to strategic deferrals and temporary operational adjustments.  

Key drivers of the variances include higher-than-anticipated vendor reliance for consulting and professional services to support renewal efforts, coupled with cost-saving measures from deferred marketing campaigns and reduced travel. The department's ability to remain under budget despite these pressures reflects strong overall fiscal discipline.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  

1. **Renewals**: Actuals of $20.26M exceeded the $18.01M budget by $2.25M (+12.5%).  
2. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $4.07M exceeded the $3.82M budget by $250.4K (+6.5%).  
3. **New Business**: Actuals of $8.69M were $248.8K (+2.9%) above the $8.44M budget, remaining on track.  
4. **Consulting**: Actuals of $2.83M exceeded the $2.67M budget by $161.2K (+6.0%).  
5. **Marketing, Technology, Facilities, Travel, and People Costs**: All reported zero spend, generating cumulative savings of $3.39M.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  

- **Renewals (+$2.25M, +12.5%)**: The overage was driven by increased reliance on external vendors to manage an unexpected surge in contract renewals for large accounts. This spike was attributed to a strategic push to lock in multi-year agreements ahead of anticipated market volatility in FY2026.  

- **Professional Services (+$250.4K, +6.5%)**: Higher-than-expected costs stemmed from additional support required for a new CRM system rollout, which involved vendor-led training and data migration services.  

- **Consulting (+$161.2K, +6.0%)**: Overages were tied to increased advisory work on compliance and regulatory updates in key markets, particularly Germany and the Nordics.  

- **Zero Spend Categories (-$3.39M)**: Marketing, Technology, Facilities, Travel, and People Costs were significantly below budget due to deferred initiatives and temporary operational freezes. For example, marketing campaigns were postponed to FY2026 to align with a broader product launch, and travel was curtailed due to the continued effectiveness of virtual client engagement.
