## **Executive Summary**  
The EMEA Sales department reported total expenses of $36.2M in FY2025 Q2, exceeding the budget of $33.2M by $3.03M (+9.1%). While year-over-year revenue growth remains strong, cost pressures have emerged across multiple categories, driven by higher-than-anticipated investments in personnel, client engagement, and technology. Key drivers include increased headcount, expanded client-facing initiatives, and unbudgeted technology upgrades. While these investments align with strategic growth objectives, tighter cost controls will be critical in the coming quarters to ensure alignment with financial targets.

---

---

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+$727K, +49.4%)**  
   The variance reflects a 12% increase in headcount to support aggressive growth targets in key markets, including Germany and the UAE. Additionally, onboarding costs and higher premiums for health insurance (Aetna: $756K) and retirement benefits (Principal Financial Group: $621K) contributed to the overage.  

2. **Renewals (+$1.04M, +6.4%)**  
   Increased client retention efforts, including expanded account management teams and enhanced service offerings, drove higher-than-budgeted costs. These efforts are expected to yield long-term revenue benefits but require closer cost monitoring.  

3. **Travel & Expense (+$234K, +39.3%)**  
   Post-pandemic travel normalization and client-facing initiatives, including Q2 roadshows and industry conferences, drove higher travel expenses.  

4. **Technology (+$136K, +45.6%)**  
   Unbudgeted investments in Workday ($647K) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($650K) were made to enhance HR and performance management systems, supporting the department’s growing workforce.
