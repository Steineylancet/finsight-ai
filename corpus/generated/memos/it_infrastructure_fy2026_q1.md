## **Executive Summary**  
The IT Infrastructure Department exceeded its FY2026 Q1 budget by $1,432,176 (+43.0%), with total actual expenses of $4,759,428 against a budget of $3,327,252. This variance was primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated People Costs (+$574,579, +48.6%) and Technology expenses (+$563,909, +38.2%). These overruns reflect strategic investments in critical infrastructure projects and an accelerated hiring timeline to support Crestwood Capital Group’s growth initiatives. While the variance is unfavorable, these expenditures align with long-term goals to enhance operational resilience and scalability.

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
### **People Costs (+$574,579, +48.6%)**  
The variance in People Costs was driven by the expedited onboarding of 15 new hires, including cloud engineers and cybersecurity specialists, to support the rollout of the new hybrid cloud platform. This hiring was originally planned for Q2 but was advanced to address immediate capacity constraints. Additionally, overtime costs increased due to extended hours required for the data center migration project.  

### **Technology (+$563,909, +38.2%)**  
Technology expenses were elevated due to upfront licensing fees for critical platforms, including Snowflake ($415,344) and Tableau ($436,657), as part of the enterprise-wide data analytics modernization initiative. Additionally, higher-than-expected usage of Amazon Web Services (AWS) ($545,610) resulted from increased demand for cloud-based applications following the Q4 2025 launch of our client-facing digital portal.  

### **Professional Services (+$168,931, +42.2%)**  
Professional Services costs exceeded budget due to consulting engagements with ServiceNow ($441,343) to streamline IT service management processes. The scope of these engagements expanded mid-quarter to address unanticipated workflow inefficiencies identified during implementation.  

### **Facilities & Occupancy (+$81,460, +48.9%)**  
The Facilities variance reflects higher-than-expected costs associated with temporary office space to accommodate the expanded IT team during the ongoing renovation of our primary data center.
