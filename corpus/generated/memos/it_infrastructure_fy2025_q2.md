## **Executive Summary**  
The IT Infrastructure Department closed FY2025 Q2 with total expenses of $5,130,719, exceeding the budget of $3,568,451 by $1,562,268 (+43.8%). This unfavorable variance was primarily driven by higher-than-anticipated technology investments, increased people costs, and expanded utilization of professional services. These cost overruns reflect strategic initiatives, including accelerated cloud migration efforts, expanded cybersecurity measures, and onboarding of critical talent to support business growth. While these investments position Crestwood Capital Group for long-term scalability, tighter cost controls and vendor contract renegotiations will be a focus in the coming quarters.

## **Key Variances & Commentary**  
### **Technology (+$692,970 / +43.4%)**  
The largest variance stemmed from accelerated spending on cloud services and software tools. Amazon Web Services (AWS) accounted for $542,080, reflecting increased data storage and compute capacity to support the ongoing digital transformation initiative. Additionally, $503,708 was allocated to Atlassian tools to enhance project management and collaboration across teams. Verizon Business costs ($507,400) were higher than forecast due to expanded bandwidth requirements to support hybrid work.  

### **People Costs (+$563,192 / +44.7%)**  
The department onboarded 15 new hires in Q2, including senior engineers and cybersecurity specialists, to address critical skill gaps. Additionally, health insurance premiums from Aetna ($424,161) rose due to higher headcount and mid-year policy adjustments.  

### **Professional Services (+$175,287 / +40.7%)**  
The variance reflects reliance on external consultants for cybersecurity audits and cloud migration support. These engagements were necessary to meet aggressive project timelines but exceeded initial estimates.  

### **Facilities & Occupancy (+$77,174 / +43.3%)**  
Office expansion in Dallas to accommodate the growing IT team resulted in higher-than-budgeted lease and utility costs.  

### **Travel & Expense (+$53,645 / +51.0%)**  
Increased travel was driven by in-person training sessions and vendor negotiations, which were not fully accounted for in the original budget.
