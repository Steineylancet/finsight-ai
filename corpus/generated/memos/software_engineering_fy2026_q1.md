### **Executive Summary**  
The Software Engineering department reported total expenses of $10.84M in FY2026 Q1, exceeding the budgeted $7.35M by $3.49M (+47.5%). This unfavorable variance was driven primarily by higher-than-expected People Costs (+$2.28M, +47.7%) and Technology expenses (+$735K, +50.7%). These overruns reflect strategic investments in talent acquisition, training, and infrastructure upgrades to support the company’s accelerated digital transformation initiatives. While these costs are significant, they align with Crestwood Capital Group’s broader growth objectives and the department’s expanded mandate to deliver critical technology projects.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **People Costs**: Actuals of $7.07M exceeded the $4.78M budget by $2.28M (+47.7%).  
2. **Technology**: Actuals of $2.19M exceeded the $1.45M budget by $735K (+50.7%).  
3. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $835K exceeded the $595K budget by $240K (+40.3%).  
4. **Travel & Expense**: Actuals of $531K exceeded the $374K budget by $157K (+42.0%).  
5. **Facilities & Occupancy**: Actuals of $221K exceeded the $146K budget by $75K (+51.6%).  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
1. **People Costs (+$2.28M, +47.7%)**  
   The largest variance stems from People Costs, which were significantly impacted by a 15% increase in headcount to support the launch of the new AI-driven Portfolio Analytics Platform. Additionally, investments in upskilling initiatives, such as Udemy Business ($2.29M), contributed to this overrun. Other major contributors include payroll processing via ADP ($2.03M) and recruitment efforts through Indeed.com ($2.03M).  

2. **Technology (+$735K, +50.7%)**  
   Technology expenses were driven by the accelerated deployment of cloud infrastructure to enhance system scalability and reliability. This included expanded licensing agreements with Workday Inc. ($2.17M) and Cornerstone OnDemand ($2.06M), both of which were necessary to support the department’s growing workforce and evolving project requirements.  

3. **Professional Services (+$240K, +40.3%)**  
   Higher-than-expected consulting fees were incurred to onboard external specialists for the Portfolio Analytics Platform. These services were critical to meeting tight project deadlines and ensuring compliance with industry standards.  

4. **Travel & Expense (+$157K, +42.0%)**  
   Increased travel was associated with cross-functional collaboration between the Software Engineering team and external vendors. This included site visits and workshops to finalize the platform architecture.  

5. **Facilities & Occupancy (+$75K, +51.6%)**  
   Facilities costs rose due to temporary office expansions and equipment upgrades to accommodate new hires.
