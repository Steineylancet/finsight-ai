### **Executive Summary**  
The Sales - APAC department closed FY2025 Q4 with total expenses of $32.29M, undershooting the budget of $35.60M by $3.31M (-9.3%). This variance was primarily driven by favorable reductions in Facilities & Occupancy, Technology, Marketing, Travel & Expense, and People Costs, all of which reported zero spend this quarter. These savings offset modest overspending in Renewals (+$724K, +4.3%), New Business (+$316K, +4.0%), Professional Services (+$150K, +4.1%), and Consulting (+$136K, +5.5%).  

While the overall financial performance is favorable, the overspend in Consulting and Professional Services warrants further investigation as it reflects higher-than-expected reliance on external vendors. Key risks include the sustainability of cost reductions in discretionary categories and the potential for vendor cost inflation in FY2026.  

---

### **Expense Performance by Category**  
1. **Renewals**: Actuals of $17.73M exceeded the $17.00M budget by $724K (+4.3%). This reflects strong customer retention efforts and higher-than-anticipated renewal volumes.  
2. **New Business**: Spending reached $8.12M, $316K (+4.0%) above budget, driven by aggressive client acquisition campaigns in high-growth markets like Singapore and Vietnam.  
3. **Professional Services**: Actuals of $3.82M were $150K (+4.1%) over budget due to increased reliance on third-party implementation teams to support enterprise clients.  
4. **Consulting**: At $2.63M, Consulting costs exceeded the $2.49M budget by $136K (+5.5%), reflecting additional advisory services for strategic market entry projects in Indonesia and Malaysia.  
5. **Facilities, Technology, Marketing, Travel, and People Costs**: All reported zero spend this quarter, contributing $4.64M in savings. These reductions were due to temporary operational shifts, including remote work policies, deferred marketing campaigns, and headcount optimization.  

---

### **Key Variances & Commentary**  
- **Consulting (+$136K, +5.5%)**: The overage was driven by higher fees from Pacific Rim Holdings (+$207K) and Singapore Commerce Group (+$69K) for market entry strategy development. These efforts are expected to yield long-term revenue growth but highlight the need for tighter vendor cost controls.  
- **Facilities & Occupancy (-$456K, -100%)**: Savings stemmed from the continued closure of regional offices in line with the company’s remote-first strategy.  
- **Travel & Expense (-$930K, -100%)**: The zero spend reflects the ongoing suspension of non-essential travel, with virtual client engagements proving effective in the region.  
- **People Costs (-$2.31M, -100%)**: Savings resulted from unfilled headcount in Q4 due to hiring freezes. While favorable in the short term, this could impact capacity to manage growth in FY2026.
